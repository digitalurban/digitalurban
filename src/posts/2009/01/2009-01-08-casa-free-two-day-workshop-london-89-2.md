Running over the 8th and 9th January 2009 in London: the first day of the event is designed to showcase CASA's use of new technologies for mapping and visualizing information about cities. The second day entails technical workshops, designed primarily for members of the <a href="http://s4.parisgeo.cnrs.fr/">S4 European Spatial Analysis network</a>, but all are welcome, the focus of the second day is agent based modelling.
