<p>As we have pondered in various posts Augmented Reality is a quick win in terms of visualisation, it is realtivly easy to implement and has a high 'wow' factor. The concept is simple, a webcam is linked up via a toolkit that is able to identify printed tags, the system then superimposes and 3D model over the scene - thus augmenting reality.<br /><br />Of course the printed tags dont have to be 'physical' they can be embedded into Virtual Worlds such as Second Life, creating a really unqiue concept of importing/moving and visualisation objects in a virtual environment.<br /><br /> Take a look at the video below from <a href="http://vimeo.com/cangurio">cristiancontini</a> for a really interesting proof of concept:<br /><br /><center><object width="480" height="362"><param name="allowfullscreen" value="true"><param name="allowscriptaccess" value="always"><param name="movie" value="http://vimeo.com/moogaloop.swf?clip_id=2868311&server=vimeo.com&show_title=1&show_byline=1&show_portrait=0&color=00ADEF&fullscreen=1"><embed src="http://vimeo.com/moogaloop.swf?clip_id=2868311&server=vimeo.com&show_title=1&show_byline=1&show_portrait=0&color=00ADEF&fullscreen=1" type="application/x-shockwave-flash" allowfullscreen="true" allowscriptaccess="always" width="480" height="362"></embed></object><br /><a href="http://vimeo.com/2868311">Augmented Mixed Reality: Second Life pops out from the Screen</a> from <a href="http://vimeo.com/cangurio">cristiancontini</a> on <a href="http://vimeo.com">Vimeo</a>.</center><br /><br />See <a href="http://www.hitl.washington.edu/artoolkit/">http://www.hitl.washington.edu/artoolkit</a>/ for download and working examples.<br /><br />See also our recent posts on <a href="http://digitalurban.blogspot.com/2008/11/papervision-augmented-reality.html">Papervision Augmented Reality</a> and <a href="http://digitalurban.blogspot.com/2008/12/augmented-reality-and-google-sketchup.html">Augmented Reality for Architecture using SketchUp</a>.<br /><a href="http://digitalurban.blogspot.com/2008/11/papervision-augmented-reality.html"></a><h3> </h3></p>
