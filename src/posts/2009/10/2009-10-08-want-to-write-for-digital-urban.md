<p>Digital Urban has until now been pretty much a one man show and as much as we would like to have four or five posts a day pressures of work are increasingly getting in the way.<br /><br />As such we thought, having gathered a notable readership, we would put out a call if any of you would like to write for the blog? We are looking for talented writers who love all stuff urban and digital, from CAD to panoramas through to timelapses and digital photography.<br /><br />Does that sound like you? Do you log into digital urban each day to read the latest developments? If so here is what you need to do:<br /><span style="font-weight: bold;"><br />Writing:<br /></span><br />Send us to two sample posts in a digital urban style, they can be about anything you want but need to be your own work. We wont use any of the samples here, it will just allow us to see if you have what we are looking for.<br /><span style="font-weight: bold;"><br />About You</span><br /><br />A few words about yourself, contact info and how much time you can commit to writing posts. This is an unpaid gig, but hopefully having written for digital urban on your CV can only be a good thing...<br /><br />Still interested? Then get in touch via the email on the <a href="http://digitalurban.blogspot.com/2008/06/contact-us.html">Contact Us</a> page.</p>
