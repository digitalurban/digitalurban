<a href="http://urbantick.blogspot.com/">UrbanTick</a> - a blog written just a few desks away from where we sit - is looking into the way a city lives and breathes. Part of the work is based on the  visualisation of GPS tracks, so linking in with another project here at <a href="http://www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/">CASA</a>, <a href="http://www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/people/person.asp?ID=28">Richard Milton</a> has written a script to import GPS tracks directly into 3D Studio Max.
