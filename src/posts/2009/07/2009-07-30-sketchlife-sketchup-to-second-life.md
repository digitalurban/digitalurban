<p>We have never quite understood why Second Life is such a closed shop when it comes down to importing models - compared to systems such as <a href="http://digitalurban.blogspot.com/2009/07/sketch-worlds-3d-max-and-sketchup.html">Sketch Worlds</a>, which we covered earlier this week, it has been a positively painful process to get any model in the environment. However, Sketchlife is a step in a right direction, allowing models to be built in SketchUp and import to Second Life.<br /><br />The video below details a simple model of the words "Hello World" being built and uploaded using Sketchlife tools. It demonstrates the process: press "Export", copy and paste the model key, drop the textures into the box, and, finally, copy and paste the build key.
