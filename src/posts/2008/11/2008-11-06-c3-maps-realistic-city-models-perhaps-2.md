<p>We met with the nice people at C3 last week and they kindly provided a walk through of their latest city modelling technology and its impressive:<br /><br /><center><object width="465" height="384"><param name="movie" value="http://www.youtube.com/v/RqtlGNtc58g&hl=en&fs=1"></param><param name="allowFullScreen" value="true"></param><param name="allowscriptaccess" value="always"></param><embed src="http://www.youtube.com/v/RqtlGNtc58g&hl=en&fs=1" type="application/x-shockwave-flash" allowscriptaccess="always" allowfullscreen="true" width="465" height="384"></embed></object></center><br /><br />The movie above provides a glimpse of the output which interestingly is not dependent on LiDAR. C3 capture the data using calibrated cameras that for every picture knows the positions at angles of capture at a high precision. This enables each pixel to have a known geographical position with decimeter accuracy and thus using a combination of images a 3D model can be produced.<br /><br />The company has just released their Oslo model and it is well worth taking a look at. <br /><br />See <a href="http://kart.sesam.no/3d/">http://kart.sesam.no/3d/</a> for a 3D flythough (Windows only at the moment) and <a href="http://www.c3technologies.com">http://www.c3technologies.com</a> for more info.<br /><br />Thanks go to Frank at the <a href="http://gearthblog.com/">Google Earth blog</a> for the email prompt to put up a post on it :)</p>
