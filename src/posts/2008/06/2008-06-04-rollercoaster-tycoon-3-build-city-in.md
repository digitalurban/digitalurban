create 3D environment - in the case of RollerCoaster Tycoon normally a theme park.<br /><br />An enthusiast known simply as 'Steven' and uploading to YouTube as <a href="http://www.youtube.com/user/alloria">alloria</a> has used the game engine to create a city.<br /><br />The end product is a flythough that some visualisation companies would be proud of:<br /><blockquote>This started out as a sandbox game, to experiment with different building types, I created one building, then another, then another, then another, and before I knew it, I had nearly filled the entire map with buildings!<br /><br />I did of course leave a small area in the middle to build a small theme park, which contains two roller coasters, a mini-golf and an Enterprise ride.<br /><br />The main coaster is a "Giga-Coaster" and is known as "Thrasher" This is the only ride that I have filmed, as the other ride "Swift" can be viewed in my "Off-Shore Theme Park" video.<br /><br />Excitement Rating: 8.25 (Very High)<br />Intensity Rating: 4.66 (Medium)<br />Nausea Rating: 2.47 (Low)<br /><br />The name "Thrasher" was chosen by my friend Matthew.<br /><br />But of course, this video is not really about the coasters, it is about what happens when I am bored, and have plenty of time to experiment with the game.<br /><br />The city contains several buildings, including:<br /><br />Some Tower Blocks.<br />A Multi-storey car-park.<br />Several ground-level Car Parks.<br />A Church with graveyard.<br />Several Houses With Gardens.<br />Several Small Blocks of flats.<br />Some Shops.<br />A Large warehouse-type shop.<br />A Bus Depot.<br />A Rest Home.<br />A Small Park With A Fountain.<br />Some office buildings.<br /><br /></blockquote>Still pictures of the city can be found at <a href="http://pjxzzt.deviantart.com/gallery/">http://pjxzzt.deviantart.com/gallery/</a></p>
