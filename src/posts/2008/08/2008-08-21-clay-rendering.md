<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="/assets/uploads/external/www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/andy/blogimages/ucl.jpg" /><br /></div><br />Rendering 3D models can be over complicated, our favorite technique is the quick and easy 'Clay' render using <a href="http://usa.autodesk.com/adsk/servlet/index?id=5659302&siteID=123112">3DMax</a>.The image above was created in <a href="http://www.sketchup.com/">SketchUp</a> and then simply imported into Max using the .3ds export.<br /><br />Once in Max take these simple steps to produce a image similar to the one above:<br /><br />1) Add a 'Skylight' to the scene (click lighting, select a Skylight and click in your scene).The Skylight can be placed anywhere as is not a direct lighting component.<br /><br />2) Hit F10 to bring up the Render Dialog and select the 'Advanced Lighting' tab, now click 'LightTracer'<br /><br />3) Render your scene.<br /><br />That's it, a quick and simple render that we often use for our images.
