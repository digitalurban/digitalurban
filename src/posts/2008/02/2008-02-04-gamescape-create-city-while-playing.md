As gameplay progresses levels are added and over time structures develop which in turn build into cityscapes that can subsequently be downloaded and imported into Google Earth.<br /><br />Is the sort of concept that does not translate well to text, the movie below provides a clearer insight:<br /><br /><center><object width="425" height="355"><param name="movie" value="http://www.youtube.com/v/ty0zz3jhcH8&rel=1"></param><param name="wmode" value="transparent"></param><embed src="http://www.youtube.com/v/ty0zz3jhcH8&rel=1" type="application/x-shockwave-flash" wmode="transparent" width="425" height="355"></embed></object></center><br /><br />The concept has been developed by 'and-or.ch'- an art group which is looking to find new ways of communication by tapping into the subconscious streams of human minds and network data. and-or was been founded in 2001 by Rene Bauer and Beat Suter, based in Zurich, Switzerland.<br /><br /><br />For more info, Google Earth examples and to download the game see: <a href="http://www.and-or.ch/gamescape/">http://www.and-or.ch/gamescape/</a></p>
