<p><a href="http://www.maptube.org/"><img style="margin: 0pt 0pt 10px 10px; float: right; cursor: pointer;" src="/assets/uploads/external/3.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/SDbQ4nbMWFI/AAAAAAAABME/ccA6fzkxARA/s320/Map1.png" alt="" id="BLOGGER_PHOTO_ID_5203576090547476562" border="0" /></a>Over the last few weeks Radio 4's PM show has been carrying out a survey on the <span>UK's</span> fears on the Credit Crunch. In association with our <span>MapTube</span> site at <a href="http://www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/"><span>CASA</span></a> listeners were asked for the first part of their postcode and to answer the following question:<br /><br /><span style="font-weight: bold;">What single factor is hurting you most about the credit crunch?</span><br /><br />Please select one of the answers below:<br /><br /><span style="font-weight: bold;">Mortgage or Rent</span><br /><span style="font-weight: bold;">Fuel</span><br /><span style="font-weight: bold;">Food Prices</span><br /><span style="font-weight: bold;">Holidays</span><br /><span style="font-weight: bold;">Other</span><br /><span style="font-weight: bold;">The Credit Crunch is not affecting me</span><br /><br />We generated a new map every 30 minutes, providing an up to date view of the nations mood.<br /><br /><a href="http://www.maptube.org/"><img style="margin: 0px auto 10px; display: block; text-align: center; cursor: pointer;" src="/assets/uploads/external/4.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/SDbRO3bMWGI/AAAAAAAABMM/X0XsOtYzyME/s400/map2.png" alt="" id="BLOGGER_PHOTO_ID_5203576472799565922" border="0" /></a><br />The results are fascinating, with over 22,000 responses 49% were most worried about fuel price with food running in second at 19%. The <span>interesting</span> part for us is to view these results both statistically and geographically as the results come in, to date the breakdown is as follows:<br /><br /><a href="http://3.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/SDvosXbMWII/AAAAAAAABMk/9OHJaaR6aeI/s1600-h/MapTubePie.png"><img style="margin: 0px auto 10px; display: block; text-align: center; cursor: pointer;" src="/assets/uploads/external/3.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/SDvosXbMWII/AAAAAAAABMk/9OHJaaR6aeI/s400/MapTubePie.png" alt="" id="BLOGGER_PHOTO_ID_5205009643256699010" border="0" /></a><br />You can view the map at <a href="http://www.maptube.org/">http://www.maptube.org</a> with full details on the <a href="http://www.bbc.co.uk/blogs/ipm/2008/04/mapping_the_credit_crunch.shtml"><span>BBC's</span> <span>iPM</span> page</a> - of note is the rural/urban split with urban areas being notably more worried about mortgage or rent payments compared to other areas of the UK.</p>
