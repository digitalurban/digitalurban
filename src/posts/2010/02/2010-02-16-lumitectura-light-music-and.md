<p>Lumitectura is a music video by <a href="http://vimeo.com/user2379378">barno</a> about the relation of light, music and architecture. The clip is defined by 3 elements.<br /><br />-One videofile, shot between 2 and 6 pm.<br />-The speed of playback of this file, which is synchronised manually to  the music.<br />-Approximately 50 different masks, which define where the underlayed movie is  going to appear on the screen.
