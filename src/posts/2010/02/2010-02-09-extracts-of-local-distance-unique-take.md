Countless fragments of existing architectural photography are merged into multi-layered shapes. The resulting collages introduce a third abstract point of view next to the original ones of architect and photographer - this is the concept behind Extracts of Local Distance.<br /><div style="clear: both; text-align: center;"><a href="http://2.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/S3FXeNVf3TI/AAAAAAAACT4/rSWoDu8TU0Y/s1600-h/localdis.jpg" style="margin-left: 1em; margin-right: 1em;"><img border="0" height="411" src="/assets/uploads/external/2.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/S3FXeNVf3TI/AAAAAAAACT4/rSWoDu8TU0Y/s640/localdis.jpg" width="600" /></a></div><br />Digital scans of analogue architectural photography form tiny pieces of a large resulting puzzle. The original pictures are being analysed and categorised according to their vanishing-points and shapes. Based on this analysis, slices are being extracted from the source image. These slices retain the information of their position corresponding to their original vanishing-point and thus form a large pool of pieces, ready to be applied to new perspectives and shapes.<br /><br />The clip below provides a look at the process:<br /><br /><center><object height="338" width="601"><param name="allowfullscreen" value="true" /><param name="allowscriptaccess" value="always" /><param name="movie" value="http://vimeo.com/moogaloop.swf?clip_id=9202755&server=vimeo.com&show_title=1&show_byline=1&show_portrait=0&color=ffffff&fullscreen=1" /><embed src="http://vimeo.com/moogaloop.swf?clip_id=9202755&server=vimeo.com&show_title=1&show_byline=1&show_portrait=0&color=ffffff&fullscreen=1" type="application/x-shockwave-flash" allowfullscreen="true" allowscriptaccess="always" width="601" height="338"></embed></object><br /><a href="http://vimeo.com/9202755">Extracts of Local Distance</a> from <a href="http://vimeo.com/stoeseltntpro">STOESELTNTPRO</a> on <a href="http://vimeo.com/">Vimeo</a>.</center><br /><br />Using the extracted image segments, it is now possible to form  collages of originally different pictures with a new common perspective.  In order to compose a collage, a perspective-grid is defined and a  lining of matching image segments is being applied. The segments are not  altered to match the frame but fitting ones are chosen from the sheer  mass of possible pieces. By defining additional keywords which describe  the content of the original photographs, the selection of segments used  for the final composition can be influenced. Thus a contextual layer is  added through the semantic linking with the source material.<br /><br /><div style="clear: both; text-align: center;"><a href="http://3.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/S3FYFSngelI/AAAAAAAACT8/ZIBQolNkkE0/s1600-h/localdistance.org_Funkhaus.jpg" style="margin-left: 1em; margin-right: 1em;"><img border="0" height="412" src="/assets/uploads/external/3.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/S3FYFSngelI/AAAAAAAACT8/ZIBQolNkkE0/s640/localdistance.org_Funkhaus.jpg" width="600" /></a></div><br />The  recompositions mix and match the views and perspectives of both the  architect and the photographer with a third, newly chosen frame. The  resulting fine-art prints are entirely unique, and represent a new take on architectural imagery.<br /><br />Head over to <a href="http://www.localdistance.org/">http://www.localdistance.org/</a> for full details, especially the <a href="http://www.localdistance.org/collages.php">results page</a> which has some stunning images.
