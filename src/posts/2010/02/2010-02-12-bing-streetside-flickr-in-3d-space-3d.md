<p>Microsoft have just just rolled out a new application that is currently in a tech  preview phase that pulls photos from <a href="http://www.flickr.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Flickr®</a>, associates them with Bing  Maps Streetside photos and then overlays them by stretching the photo to  form fit where in the world it belongs. The new application called <a href="http://www.bing.com/maps/explore/#5003/0.6044=&o=&a=0/5872/style=a&lat=42.906722&lon=-119.156436&z=6&pid=5874">Streetside  Photos</a> is currently available in Seattle, San Francisco and  Vancouver (Canada) to view your Flickr photos in a  whole new way. We gave it a spin this morning and captured the video below, it has huge potential to view historic photos and crowd source the latest imagery.<br /><br />Note how the 3D transitions between scenes and how the photos become part of the panorama:<br /><br /><object height="345" width="60"><param name="movie" value="http://www.youtube.com/v/rzDBooFSDU8&hl=en_US&fs=1&"><param name="allowFullScreen" value="true"><param name="allowscriptaccess" value="always"><embed src="http://www.youtube.com/v/rzDBooFSDU8&hl=en_US&fs=1&" type="application/x-shockwave-flash" allowscriptaccess="always" allowfullscreen="true" height="345" width="600"></embed></object><br /><br />See the <a href="http://www.bing.com/community/blogs/maps/archive/2010/02/11/new-bing-maps-application-streetside-photos.aspx">post over on the Microsoft site</a> for full details on Streetside or jump direct to the <a href="http://www.bing.com/maps/explore/#5003/0.6044=&o=&a=0/5872/style=a&lat=42.906722&lon=-119.156436&z=6&pid=5874">Streetside  Photos </a>application.</p>
