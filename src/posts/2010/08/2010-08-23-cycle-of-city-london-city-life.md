<p>The movie below is a degree short piece written, filmed and edited by James Varty & Andy Hippisley of <a href="http://vimeo.com/lastinline">Last in Line Productions</a>.<br /><br />Filmed on Sony z1 and edited in Final Cut Pro it portrays life in the city and the endless cycle of travel, work, sleep:<br /><br /><center><iframe src="http://player.vimeo.com/video/14353549?title=0&byline=0&portrait=0" width="640" height="360" frameborder="0"></iframe></center><br /><br />The cycle is a bit <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reggie_Perrin">Reggie Perrin</a>, but deep in there are some interesting questions on how cities have a natural rhythm and cycles linked to economics, geography, societal constraints etc. For more on these lines of thinking head over to <a href="http://www.urbantick.blogspot.com">urbantick</a> for a look into research on cycles and rhythms with a focus on space-time related technologies.</p>
