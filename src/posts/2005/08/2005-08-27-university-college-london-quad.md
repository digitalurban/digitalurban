<p><img src="/assets/uploads/external/www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/andy/blogimages/uclquad.jpg" /><br /><br />University College London was founded on February 11th 1826, the third University in the United Kingdom after Oxford and Cambridge. <br /><br />The Universities main building, pictured in this panorama, was designed by William Wilkins and constructed between 1827 and 1828. The building is centered on a Corinthian portico which is reminiscent of the British Museum, (constructed a couple of years earlier). <br /><br />After the building of University College London Wilkins went to design the National Gallery in Trafalgar Square.<br /><br /><a href="http://www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/andy/qt/uclquad.html">View the Quicktime panorama of The University of London Quad, London </a>(2.6mb).<br /><br />The raw images behind the panoramas can be viewed at <a href="http://www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/andy/photographs/">the Gallery</a></p>
