<p><img src="/assets/uploads/external/www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/andy/blogimages/centre.jpg" /><br /><br />Centre Point stands at 117m on the junction of Oxford Street and Charing Cross Road in London. The building, completed in 1967, is made out of pre-cast concrete and at the time held the honour of the worlds tallest concrete tower. The building is a controversial landmark in London with its unique architecture.
