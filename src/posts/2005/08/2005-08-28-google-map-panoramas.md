<p><img src="/assets/uploads/external/www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/andy/blogimages/googlemap.jpg" /><br /><br />With thanks to <a href="http://www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/kampanart/home.htm">Kampanart Piyathamrongchai </a> (Known as Meo) from our lab the <a href="http://www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/">Centre for Advanced Spatial Analysis</a>, at University College London, we have been able to embed the panoramas from the blog into Google Maps.<br /><br />Meo hacked about with the code to enable quicktime to be pulled off our server and overlaid on top of the standard Google Map interface. It is possible to view three types of map - standard, satellite and hybrid.<br /><br /> - edit - The system works happily in Firefox but seems to have some issues Explorer with the Quicktime not showing. We have added a full screen mode which maximises the map according to your browser size, the panoramas recently added to the blog will appear on the map over the coming days. - edit - <br /><br /><a href="http://www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/andy/londonmap.html">View the Google Map</a> or<br /><br /><a href="http://www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/andy/londonmaptest.html">View the Google Map Full Screen</a></p>
