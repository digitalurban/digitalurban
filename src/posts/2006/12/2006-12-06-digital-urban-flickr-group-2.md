So far we have 12 images online and welcome the first member  - James Bell of  the excellent panorama site <a href="http://www.jamesbells.com">jamesbells.com</a><br /><br />Its easy to join the group, simply login or set up a <a href="http://www.flickr.com/">Flickr</a> account and <a href="http://www.flickr.com/groups/63173595@N00/">click here to join the DigitalUrban Group</a>.<br /><br />There is also the ability to post questions or thoughts in the discussion link. If you have any photographs you would like to share we encourage you to join - we might even set up a monthly prize for best urban image :)</p>
