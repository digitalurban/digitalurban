<p><a href="http://photos1.blogger.com/blogger2/604/1219/1600/Natwest2.jpg"><img style="FLOAT: left; MARGIN: 0px 10px 10px 0px; CURSOR: hand" alt="" src="/assets/uploads/external/photos1.blogger.com/blogger2/604/1219/320/Natwest2.jpg" border="0" /></a>Tower 42 is the tallest building in the City of London at 183 metres compared to 179.8 metres for its neighboring <a href="http://digitalurban.blogspot.com/2006/09/swiss-re-london-high-definition-ranging.html">30 St Mary Axe</a>.<br /><br />Built in 1980 by Architects Seifert and Partners, Tower 42 was originally home to the Natwest Bank with rumours that its unique shape mirrored the Natwest's logo at the time. This seems to be however an urban myth, the building is now an office complex with a restaurant on the 42nd floor offering views of London.<br /><br /><br />The panorama is captured using the same bracketing technique as the previous <a href="http://digitalurban.blogspot.com/2006/09/swiss-re-london-high-definition-ranging.html">Swiss Re </a>image.<br /><br />We also feature Tower 42 in Google Earth as a 3D Model, you can <a href="http://digitalurban.blogspot.com/2006/01/google-earth-tower-42-and-swiss-re.html">read the post on it and download the KMZ from here</a>.<br /><br /><br /><img style="DISPLAY: block; MARGIN: 0px auto 10px; CURSOR: hand; TEXT-ALIGN: center" alt="" src="/assets/uploads/external/photos1.blogger.com/blogger2/604/1219/400/Natwest1_copy.jpg" border="0" /><br />You can <a href="http://www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/andy/qt/Natwest.html">view the panorama in QuickTime VR</a> (3.4Mb).</p>
