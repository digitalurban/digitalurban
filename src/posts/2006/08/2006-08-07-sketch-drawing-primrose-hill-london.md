So the panorama that was going to be featured here of Primrose Hill failed, however due to the lighting conditions it did lend itself to a few Photoshop filters aimed at creating a sketch view of the skyline.<br /><br />Obtaining sketches of the London skyline is not easy yet, in our view at least, they would make a highly desirable  framed print on our office wall.
