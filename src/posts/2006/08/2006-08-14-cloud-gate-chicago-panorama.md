<p><a href="http://photos1.blogger.com/blogger/835/752/1600/Sphereround2.jpg"><img style="display:block; margin:0px auto 10px; text-align:center;cursor:pointer; cursor:hand;" src="/assets/uploads/external/photos1.blogger.com/blogger/835/752/400/Sphereround2.jpg" border="0" alt="" /></a><br />London is far too overcast today for panoramas so we thought we would post a shot we took on a conference trip to Chicago late last year of Cloud Gate in Millenium Park.<br /><br />Cloud Gate is British artist Anish Kapoor's first public outdoor work installed in the United States. The 110-ton elliptical sculpture is forged of a seamless series of highly polished stainless steel plates, which reflect the city's famous skyline and the clouds above. A 12-foot-high arch provides a "gate" to the concave chamber beneath the sculpture, inviting visitors to touch its mirror-like surface and see their image reflected back from a variety of perspectives. <br /><br /><a href="http://photos1.blogger.com/blogger/835/752/1600/Sphereround1.jpg"><img style="display:block; margin:0px auto 10px; text-align:center;cursor:pointer; cursor:hand;" src="/assets/uploads/external/photos1.blogger.com/blogger/835/752/400/Sphereround1.jpg" border="0" alt="" /></a><br />Inspired by liquid mercury, the sculpture is among the largest of its kind in the world, measuring 66-feet long by 33-feet high.
