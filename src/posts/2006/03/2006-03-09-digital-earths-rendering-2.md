<p><img src="/assets/uploads/external/www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/andy/blogimages/earthtalk2.jpg" /><br /><br />JC Francois's site <a href="http://www.noirextreme.com/earth">NoirExtreme</a> was a life saver when i found i had to step into give a lecture to the British Council on Global Warming.<br /><br />This obviously requires a highly polished visualisation of the earth linking data relevant to climate change and thus demonstrating how new technologies cna be used to inform the public. Its a no brainer to use Google Earth but for a intro movie 3D Max can produce a more professional output.<br /><br />Francois has a <a href="http://www.noirextreme.com/earth">tutorial on creating a digital earth</a> complete with lens flare - allowing a intro movie to be rendered overnight in time for the mornings talk.<br /><br /><img src="/assets/uploads/external/www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/andy/blogimages/earthtalk1.jpg" /><br /><br />Google Earth is impressive but for pure visualisation you cant beat a good rendering in 3DMax.</p>
