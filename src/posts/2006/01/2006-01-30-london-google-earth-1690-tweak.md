<p><img src="/assets/uploads/external/www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/andy/blogimages/16903.jpg" /><br /><br />The image overlay of London 1690 has received a lot of interest. So much so that we now have a project at the lab to put more of these up as soon as copyright is cleared.<br /><br />Frank Taylor from the excellent <a href="http://www.gearthblog.com/">Google Earth Blog </a> has sent us a tweak to the original file so that the Tate Modern now sits on the banks of the Thames rather than slightly in it due to a misalign. Thanks for that Frank. <br /><br /><img src="/assets/uploads/external/www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/andy/blogimages/16902.jpg" /><br /><br />A number of key landmarks which were around in London 1690 and other overlays which are coming shortly will be added. Hopefully making it a good resource of the teaching of History and Geography.<br /><br />You can download the kmz file to fly into the overlay in Google Earth <a href="http://www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/andy/kmz/London1690.kmz">here</a></p>
