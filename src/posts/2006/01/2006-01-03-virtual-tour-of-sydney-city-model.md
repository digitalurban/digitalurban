<p><img src="/assets/uploads/external/www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/andy/blogimages/sydney.jpg" /><br /><br />Peter Murphy of the excellent <a href="http://www.mediavr.com/blog/">Panoramic VR Blog</a> has put online a virtual tour around the acrylic block model of Sydney. Covering over 1000 buildings and 10 square kilometres of the city it is similar in scope to the architects models that exist in many of the worlds cities. <br /><br />What is unique here is that at first glimpse it is difficult to tell if the model is real or virtual. It is of course real and well worth a look..<br /><br /><a href="http://www.cityofsydney.nsw.gov.au/customshouse/visiting/CityModel.html">View the Virtual Sydney Model Tour.</a>.</p>
