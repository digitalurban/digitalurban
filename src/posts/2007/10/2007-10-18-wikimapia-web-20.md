A few weeks ago we started hearing the term Wikitecture of which more on that soon and now its <a href="http://wikimapia.org/">WikiMapia</a>.<br /><br />While WikiMapia has been around for a while with over 4 million places layered onto Google Maps it is interesting in terms of the time required for Web 2.0 services to enter general conversation - at least in the social circles we mix in...<br /><br />WikiMapia is a great concept and one that further enhances the use of Google Maps, it is also a prime example of the new Network Link feature just released by Google.<br /><br />Read more about <a href="http://www.ogleearth.com/2007/10/network_links_c_1.html">Network Links in Google Maps via Ogle Earth</a> and see <a href="http://wikimapia.org/">WikiMapia</a> to start browsing or adding your own Wiki style info.</p>
