Currently being shown at the Third International Conference for E-<span>Social</span> Science is World Mapper - World Mapper is a collaborative project from the <span>University</span> of Sheffield contains 366 <span>unique</span> views of the world.<br /><br />The image below is indicative of the project detailing population:<br /><br /><a href="http://2.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/Rwjp5YE2mJI/AAAAAAAAAto/ET9UBg1Tt3c/s1600-h/2.png"><img style="margin: 0px auto 10px; display: block; text-align: center; cursor: pointer;" src="/assets/uploads/external/2.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/Rwjp5YE2mJI/AAAAAAAAAto/ET9UBg1Tt3c/s400/2.png" alt="" id="BLOGGER_PHOTO_ID_5118598148431190162" border="0" /></a><br />In Spring 2000 world population estimates reached 6 billion; that is 6 thousand million. The distribution of the earth's population is shown in this map.<p>India, China and Japan appear large on the map because they have large populations. Panama, Namibia and Guinea-Bissau have small populations so are barely visible on the map. </p><p>Population is very weakly related to land area. However, Sudan which is geographically the largest country in Africa, has a smaller population than Nigeria, Egypt, Ethiopia, Democratic Republic of Congo, South Africa and Tanzania.</p><a href="http://www.worldmapper.org/index.html">Take a look at the World Mapper site</a>
