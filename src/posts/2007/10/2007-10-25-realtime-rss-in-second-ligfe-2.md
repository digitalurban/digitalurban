<p><a href="http://1.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/RyC1aReNugI/AAAAAAAAAw8/f7YpIwuGwxU/s1600-h/slrss.jpg"><img style="margin: 0px auto 10px; display: block; text-align: center; cursor: pointer;" src="/assets/uploads/external/1.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/RyC1aReNugI/AAAAAAAAAw8/f7YpIwuGwxU/s400/slrss.jpg" alt="" id="BLOGGER_PHOTO_ID_5125295838918982146" border="0" /></a>As part of our continuing work to investigate real time or near real time feeds into Second Life we now have a RSS feed running on a text board within our section of Second Nature Island.<br /><br />Joel at CASA has written a script to link each prim to a text character allowing any feed to be displayed on a ticker board. At the moment the board is rigged up to the BBC feed, updating every 20 seconds with the latest news on a 24 x 10 display.<br /><br />At the moment it only supports RSS but we are working on a Atom related version.<br /><br />If you have Second Life installed you can visit us by <a href="http://slurl.com/secondlife/Second%20Nature%202/202/192/117">clicking here</a> - you will get a red arrow pointing skywards, simply fly up to our section of Second Nature island.</p>
