<p>The end of our third day colonising the skies above Second Nature Island in Second Life and we now have a number ways to visualise both local, city and global data sets.<br /><br /><a href="http://4.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/RrNPqlo4pCI/AAAAAAAAAgw/d8-_i7Jed0k/s1600-h/MapCap.JPG"><img style="margin: 0px auto 10px; display: block; text-align: center; cursor: pointer;" src="/assets/uploads/external/4.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/RrNPqlo4pCI/AAAAAAAAAgw/d8-_i7Jed0k/s400/MapCap.JPG" alt="" id="BLOGGER_PHOTO_ID_5094503196562859042" border="0" /></a>Pictured above are two datasets for London complete with a key and information point which when clicked display additional background information on the data provided. While early days it does lend itself to a simple representation of data sets within the collaborative environment which is Second Life.<br /><br /><a href="http://4.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/RrNQKlo4pDI/AAAAAAAAAg4/KuMVDQz-OxQ/s1600-h/TrafCap.JPG"><img style="margin: 0px auto 10px; display: block; text-align: center; cursor: pointer;" src="/assets/uploads/external/4.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/RrNQKlo4pDI/AAAAAAAAAg4/KuMVDQz-OxQ/s400/TrafCap.JPG" alt="" id="BLOGGER_PHOTO_ID_5094503746318672946" border="0" /></a><br />There are now six 'Urban Bubbles' on Second Nature Island, each with a staircase leading to a viewing platform. These panoramic spheres provide a quick way to visualise local locations, data points can be subsequently mapped inside the scenes to access any relevant information.<br /><br /><a href="http://3.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/RrNQoVo4pEI/AAAAAAAAAhA/zjY7RoKoOXM/s1600-h/GlobeCapt.JPG"><img style="margin: 0px auto 10px; display: block; text-align: center; cursor: pointer;" src="/assets/uploads/external/3.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/RrNQoVo4pEI/AAAAAAAAAhA/zjY7RoKoOXM/s400/GlobeCapt.JPG" alt="" id="BLOGGER_PHOTO_ID_5094504257419781186" border="0" /></a>Finally pictured above we have Global Gross Domestic Product mapped onto a globe. Using an alpha map along with fake shadows on the display table it makes for a simple but visually compelling way to look at global data sets. We hope to add various layers to the globe over the coming weeks.<br /><br />Drop in and see us in Second Life - <a href="http://slurl.com/secondlife/Second%20Nature/232/169/116">http://slurl.com/secondlife/Second Nature/232/169/116</a></p>
