<p>Spatial and geographic awareness are kind of central to both our blog and the research in our Lab at <a href="http://www.casa.ucl.ac.uk">CASA</a>. As such it came as a slight shock to hear the statistic that 1 in 5 American citizens are unable to point out the United States on a map.<br /><br />To be honest this cannot be true can it?
