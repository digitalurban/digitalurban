A quick google search later however shows that we have been well and truly beaten to it - there are a number of examples out there ranging from attaching a N95 to a remote control plane to the more traditional kite aerial photography.<br /><br />Scott Janousek has posted a video online of his attempts to attach his N95 to a kite using duct tape:<br /><center><br /></center><br /><br />The N95 has built in GPS - if you then link the GPS with its 5 mega pixel camera in timelapse mode it holds the possibility of good quality aerial imagery.<br /><br />See <a href="http://www.eirikso.com/2007/06/29/a-flying-nokia-n95/">Scotts site</a> for more info on his kite flying and <a href="http://pictearth.ning.com/">Pictearth</a> for advanced image acquisition using lightweight, easy to use UAV, GPS, high resolution sensor, live communication and Pict'Earth software.<br /><br />Sometime during the week we may try flying a kite in Regents Park, London so if you see someone duct taping their phone to a kite it'll be us...</p>
