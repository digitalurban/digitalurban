Double click to zoom in and drag to pan:<br /><br /><center><iframe src="http://www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/googlemaps/TUBEIMAGE.html" frameborder="0" width="470" scrolling="no" height="370"></iframe></center><br /><br />The final cleaned up version will be online shortly.<br /><br />You can also <a href="http://www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/googlemaps/TUBEIMAGE.html">view the image full screen (recommended)</a>. <br /><br />Thanks go to Simon Clarke who produced the original geographic tube map. Simon's site is currently under reconstruction in the meantime take a look at other <a href="http://owen.massey.net/tubemaps.html">London Underground diagrams</a>.</p>
