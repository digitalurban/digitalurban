<p>A YouTube member known as <a href="http://uk.youtube.com/user/lhetherington">lhetherington</a> has uploaded an interesting movie based around the concept of abstract reconstruction of recorded information.<br /><br />Paraphrasing lhetherington's introduction - instead of a traditional 3D flythrough movie he/she decided to use a 3D environment (<a href="http://www.secondlife.com">Secondlife</a>) for presenting the artwork based on data collected from the Torpoint Ferry (Plymouth, UK).
