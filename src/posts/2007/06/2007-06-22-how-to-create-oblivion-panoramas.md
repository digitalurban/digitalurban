<p><a href="http://4.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/RnvhTxY7YfI/AAAAAAAAAa4/cCXZKxHRwXY/s1600-h/obpano.jpg"><img style="margin: 0px auto 10px; display: block; text-align: center; cursor: pointer;" src="/assets/uploads/external/4.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/RnvhTxY7YfI/AAAAAAAAAa4/cCXZKxHRwXY/s400/obpano.jpg" alt="" id="BLOGGER_PHOTO_ID_5078900734582022642" border="0" /></a><br />A reader of the blog going by the name of <span>digitdaily</span> has sent us in some great instructions on how to create full 360x180 degree panoramas from Oblivion.<br /><br />Digit decided to give the process a try while waiting for our part 2 of the Importing <span>SketchUp</span>/3D Max into the Oblivion Engine tutorial. The news is that we have been kind of busy and a move of machines to vista has not helped the process. We are aware that part 2 is the main section of the tutorial and we have cleared next Wednesday morning to put it in place. Till then <a href="http://digitalurban.blogspot.com/2007/05/tutorial-1-importing-sketchup3d-max.html">see Part 1</a> to create your first world.<br /><br /><a href="http://2.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/RnvhYRY7YgI/AAAAAAAAAbA/BIF9jwKL1dc/s1600-h/oblivionpano1.jpg"><img style="margin: 0px auto 10px; display: block; text-align: center; cursor: pointer;" src="/assets/uploads/external/2.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/RnvhYRY7YgI/AAAAAAAAAbA/BIF9jwKL1dc/s400/oblivionpano1.jpg" alt="" id="BLOGGER_PHOTO_ID_5078900811891433986" border="0" /></a><br />To create a panorama from <span>Oblivion</span> all you have to do is:<br /><br />1) Open the Oblivion.<span>ini</span> file.<br /><br />2)  Change <span>bAllowScreenShot</span>=0 to <span>bAllowScreenShot</span>=1 and save.<br /><br />3) In game press the ~ button and type in <span>tm</span> and enter then ~ again.  This  turns off the menu so you can just pan and hit the print screen button.<br /><br />All the  screen captures will appear in the Oblivion folder as .<span>bmp</span> files.<br /><br />4) Convert the images to .<span>jpgs</span><br /><br />5) Drag the images into <a href="http://www.cs.ubc.ca/~mbrown/autostitch/autostitch.html"><span>AutoStitch</span>.</a><br /><br />Note - <span>AutoStitch</span> is a free panorama <span>stitcher</span>, you can also use <a href="http://www.ptgui.com/"><span>PTGui</span></a> or <a href="http://stitcher.realviz.com/"><span>Stitcher</span></a> by <span>RealViz</span>.<br /><br />Thanks go to <span>digitdaily</span> for the tips, if you create any panoramas or would like any tips on stitching then let us know.<br /><br />You can purchase a  copy of Oblivion <a href="http://astore.amazon.com/digiurba-20/104-7240808-6091906?%5Fencoding=UTF8&node=2">direct from our Amazon Store</a> or any good games outlet.</p>
