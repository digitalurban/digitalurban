<p><a href="http://4.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/R0VQb--FycI/AAAAAAAAA5U/EHKjzjkvo6k/s1600-h/Flood1.jpg"><img style="margin: 0px auto 10px; display: block; text-align: center; cursor: pointer;" src="/assets/uploads/external/4.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/R0VQb--FycI/AAAAAAAAA5U/EHKjzjkvo6k/s400/Flood1.jpg" alt="" id="BLOGGER_PHOTO_ID_5135599391776033218" border="0" /></a><br />Climate change and its effects on cities is an increasingly hot topic, as such its not surprising that it is a topic being tapped into by movie makers. While climate change may not be a good thing we do like the movies about cities and the digital effects that come with them. The Flood, a movie directed by Tony Mitchell and based on the novel by Richard Doyle, develops a scenario whereby London is submerged under 20ft of flood-water.<br /><br />The trailer below provides a glimpse of London that any digital effects creator would be proud of:<br /><br /><center><object height="355" width="425"><param name="movie" value="http://www.youtube.com/v/PBWhO5iOq74&rel=1"><param name="wmode" value="transparent"><embed src="http://www.youtube.com/v/PBWhO5iOq74&rel=1" type="application/x-shockwave-flash" wmode="transparent" height="355" width="425"></embed></object></center><br /><br />In order to reassure the public in light of the film <a href="http://www.environment-agency.gov.uk/">The Environment Agency</a>, perhaps surprisingly, <a href="http://www.environment-agency.gov.uk/news/1832194?lang=_e&region=&projectstatus=&theme=&subject=&searchfor=&topic=&area=&month">issued a statement</a>. The agency reassure that the possibility of London’s defence structures succumbing to a major flood is currently estimated at having a 1:2000 or 0.05 per cent chance of occurring.<br /><br />The last major flood was a 1:300 event in 1953 and it was this event that led to the construction of the Barrier. Tony Mitchell however <a href="http://www.bbc.co.uk/films/2007/10/22/flood_2007_dvd_review.shtml">states</a> that the film was 'scientifically accurate'.<br /><br />The movie was released in August and is <a href="http://www.amazon.co.uk/Flood-Jessalyn-Gilsig/dp/B000VIRD9C">now available on DVD</a>, while the reviews were mixed its a good watch even if only for the city effects....</p>
