<p><a href="http://2.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/RiO134-pfII/AAAAAAAAAR0/Unlt5hKZbF4/s1600-h/Capture.JPG"><img style="margin: 0pt 0pt 10px 10px; float: right; cursor: pointer;" src="/assets/uploads/external/2.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/RiO134-pfII/AAAAAAAAAR0/Unlt5hKZbF4/s320/Capture.JPG" alt="" id="BLOGGER_PHOTO_ID_5054083178632412290" border="0" /></a><br />It took a visit to <a href="http://www.gearthblog.com/">Franks Google Earth blog</a> and then onwards to<a href="http://geography2.blogspot.com/"> Alan's Geography 2.0 Virtual Globes</a> to find out the details - we will be showing work at the Spatial Technology Gallery, a special event at the 2007 Association of American Geographers Annual Meeting.<br /><br />Details taken from <a href="http://geography2.blogspot.com/">Geography 2.0</a> are:<br /><br />When:<br />Wednesday, April 18, 2007, 7:00pm-9:00pm,<br /><br />Where:<br />Conference Room Yosemite B, Hilton San Francisco, located at 333<br />O'Farrell Street San Francisco, CA 94102 -<a href="http://maps.google.com/maps/ms?ie=UTF8&hl=en&amp;msa=0&msid=107776304365755978893.00000111c0b03ac4f91b8">View Location on My Maps</a>.<br /><br />What:<br />On Wednesday evening, a select group of researchers from academia, government labs, and industry have been invited to demonstrate their latest, cutting-edge spatially-enabled technologies. The format will be simultaneous demos--a mix of science fair, test drive, and show-and-tell. This organic event will provide an opportunity to examine and discuss the technology and incubating ideas that will be impacting in coming weeks, months, and years, with the pioneering minds that are making them. We anticipate topics spanning virtual globes, augmented reality, open source mapping projects, internet GIS, environmental simulation and prediction, map mashups, and mobile spatial data collection.<br /><br />This "after hours" event is open to anyone interested in spatial technology, so you will not need to register for the AAG meeting to attend. If you want to participate in any other aspects of the AAG meeting, however, you would need to register and pay like normal.<br /><br /><br />Who:<br />--ESRI<br />--Microsoft<br />--NASA World Wind<br />--Urban Mapping LLC<br />--CASA University College London<br />--Center for Fire Research and Outreach (Berkeley)<br />--UCSB Center for Nanotechnology in Society and UCSB Geography<br />--Dave Lemberg (Western Michigan)<br />--Jesse Rouse and Sue Bergeron (WVU/Very Spatial)<br />--Johannes Schoening (Muenster)<br />--Brian Tomaszewski (Penn State)<br />--Dawn Wright and Andre Skupin (Place and Spaces Project)<br />--Chuanrong Zhang (Kent State)</p>
