<p>I am writing a book chapter at the moment on the 'Visual City' and got thinking about our previous post on <a href="http://digitalurban.blogspot.com/2007/03/urban-twitters.html">Urban Twitters</a> and how the city could be used to visualise such communication. Turns out that by using a computer controlled UV laser projector and phosphorescent panels on a building wall it can, and indeed has been, done:<br /><object class codebase="http://download.macromedia.com/pub/shockwave/cabs/flash/swflash.cab#version=6,0,29,0" height="470" width="392"><br />        <param name="movie" value="dreamingwall.swf"><br />        <param name="quality" value="high"><br />        <embed src="http://www.conceptualdevices.com/ENG/dreamingwall/index_files/dreamingwall.swf" quality="high" pluginspage="http://www.macromedia.com/go/getflashplayer" type="application/x-shockwave-flash" height="470" width="392">  </embed>     </object><br />The Dreaming Wall, movie pictured above, is a project running in a historical square in Milan, conceived as a info-forum it randomly displays sms messages sent by standing in the square or via the Internet - similar in many ways to <a href="http://twitter.com/">Twitter</a>.<br /><br />Take a look at the <a href="http://www.conceptualdevices.com/ENG/dreamingwall/index.htm">Dreaming Wall project</a> which can be seen at the <a href="http://www.galleriacontemporaneo.it/">Galleria Contemporaneo, Mestre, Italy</a> until March 31st.</p>
