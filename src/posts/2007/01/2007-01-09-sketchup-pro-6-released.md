Sketchup is by far our favaourite peice of software to date and is the only design tool which we can clearly say is a joy to use. Version 6 has a numbe rof new features, including:<span style="font-size:100%;"><br /></span><ul><li><span align="left" style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:100%;color:#666666;"><b>Photo Match</b> - Use photos to build accurate 3D models much  more easily than starting from scratch.</span>  </li><li><span style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:100%;color:#666666;"><b>Styles</b> - Personalize your model by creating your own Style with  the new display</span><span style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:100%;color:#666666;"> settings, Sketchy Effects and Watermarks. </span> </li><li><span align="left" style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:100%;color:#666666;"><b>Sketchy Effects</b> - Renders edges to look as if they were  drawn by hand. Choose from a variety of line strokes such as Graphite on Bond,  Loose Marker and Shakey Pen.</span>  </li><li><span style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:100%;color:#666666;"><b>Watermarks</b> - Place 2D images behind or in front of models.</span>  </li><li><span align="left" style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:100%;color:#666666;"><b>Fog</b> - Adds atmospheric haze and a sense of depth to your  model.</span>  </li><li><span align="left" style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:100%;color:#666666;"><b>3D Text</b> - Create signs and letters and choose from simple  outlines, filled surfaces, and fully extruded 3D text.</span> </li></ul> <p><span align="left" style="font-family:Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;font-size:100%;color:#666666;">You can learn more about the <a href="http://atlast.mail.zingstudios.net/mail/click.php?314161365274975719">new  features</a> in SketchUp Pro 6 as well as taking a look at the new <a href="http://atlast.mail.zingstudios.net/mail/click.php?314161367274975719">video  tutorials</a>. At the moment the <a href="http://www.sketchup.com">SketchUp site</a> seems to be offline but as soon its back we look forward to giving the new features a spin.</span></p>
