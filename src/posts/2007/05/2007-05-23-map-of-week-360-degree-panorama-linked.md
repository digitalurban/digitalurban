<p>Linking panoramas with Google Maps has been a aim  of many panorama creators - we have added overlays within Google Maps via Quicktime, complete with interaction - but its always nice to have a marker showing where you are looking (see our <a href="http://digitalurban.blogspot.com/2005/11/london-google-map-panoramas-community.html">Google Map Panorama Post</a> for a working example, as pictured below).<br /><br /><br /><a href="http://www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/andy/blogimages/communitywalkupdate.jpg"><img style="margin: 0px auto 10px; display: block; text-align: center; cursor: pointer; width: 400px;" src="/assets/uploads/external/www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/andy/blogimages/communitywalkupdate.jpg" alt="" border="0" /></a><br /><a href="http://www.blogger.com/Map.movin-360.com%20">Map Movin 360</a> takes this a stage further by linking panoramic video and Google Maps, the result is the ability to pan around the video with  Google Maps updating  according to your viewpoint.<br /><br /><a href="http://1.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/RlQfplXQflI/AAAAAAAAAVo/wNrQn2xk3rU/s1600-h/Capture.JPG"><img style="margin: 0px auto 10px; display: block; text-align: center; cursor: pointer;" src="/assets/uploads/external/1.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/RlQfplXQflI/AAAAAAAAAVo/wNrQn2xk3rU/s400/Capture.JPG" alt="" id="BLOGGER_PHOTO_ID_5067710279963410002" border="0" /></a><br /><br /><a href="http://map.movin-360.com/">Map Movin 360</a> is well worth a look - it would be interesting to see if the same could be done with QuickTime panoramas or the emerging flash panorama viewers.<br /><br />Thanks go to <a href="http://googlemapsmania.blogspot.com/">Google Maps Mania</a> for highlighting this one..</p>
