<p>As we <a href="http://digitalurban.blogspot.com/2007/05/google-maps-street-view-360-panoramas.html">posted a couple of days</a> ago Google have added panoramas to their Google Maps services with the launch of Street View. Currently available for Silicon Valley, <span>Las</span> Vegas, Miami, Denver and New York new areas will be added over the coming months.<br /><br />The <span>YouTube</span> movie below from Google explains the concept:<br /><br /><center><object height="350" width="425"><param name="movie" value="http://www.youtube.com/v/91wuBqlny50"><param name="wmode" value="transparent"><embed src="http://www.youtube.com/v/91wuBqlny50" type="application/x-shockwave-flash" wmode="transparent" height="350" width="425"></embed></object></center><br /><br /><a href="http://2.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/Rl7vWVXQf6I/AAAAAAAAAYQ/AmL5alBiBrM/s1600-h/Capture.JPG"><img style="margin: 0pt 0pt 10px 10px; float: right; cursor: pointer;" src="/assets/uploads/external/2.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/Rl7vWVXQf6I/AAAAAAAAAYQ/AmL5alBiBrM/s200/Capture.JPG" alt="" id="BLOGGER_PHOTO_ID_5070753397436612514" border="0" /></a>Taking a closer look the data is provided by <a href="http://www.immersivemedia.com/aboutus/aboutus.php?pageID=7"><span>Immersive</span> Media Corp</a>, a digital video imaging company founded in 1994 captured in what is basically streaming video linked to a GPS. Using an 11 lens camera system (pictured right, see '<a href="http://www.prdifferently.com/2007/05/what_the_immers.html">What is Immersive Media</a>' for more info) data is streamed in and merged in <span>realtime</span> to provide panoramic video. See the<a href="http://demos.immersivemedia.com/"> <span>Immersive</span> Demo page</a> for examples.<br /><br />We used an early unit five years ago to capture panoramas around London, the system is easy to set up, basically the camera is mounted via a suction cup to the roof of a car and the data saved via a small form PC.<br /><br />Pictured below is the car used to capture the images for Google:<br /><br /><a href="http://www.prdifferently.com/images/2007/05/31/immersive1.jpg"><img style="margin: 0px auto 10px; display: block; text-align: center; cursor: pointer; width: 320px;" src="http://www.prdifferently.com/images/2007/05/31/immersive1.jpg" alt="" border="0" /></a>The <span>YouTube</span> movie below highlights the use Google Street View in San <span>Francisco</span> - note the ability to zoom but also the numerous problems with stitching and image matching:<br /><br /><center><object height="350" width="425"><param name="movie" value="http://www.youtube.com/v/fLvL9m93aFo"><param name="wmode" value="transparent"><embed src="http://www.youtube.com/v/fLvL9m93aFo" type="application/x-shockwave-flash" wmode="transparent" height="350" width="425"></embed></object></center><br /><br />While the <span>Immersive</span> system is <span>indeed</span> impressive it does suffer from problems due to cameras going out of alignment and changing lighting levels between each of the 11 views. As such although it allows the rapid capture of cities - all you need to do is drive each route -  the quality sometimes leaves a lot to be desired when compared with the more traditional panoramic techniques.<br /><br />The image below highlights these issues:<br /><br /><a href="http://3.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/Rl7filXQf5I/AAAAAAAAAYI/G5hcv7ushek/s1600-h/Capture.JPG"><img style="margin: 0px auto 10px; display: block; text-align: center; cursor: pointer;" src="/assets/uploads/external/3.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/Rl7filXQf5I/AAAAAAAAAYI/G5hcv7ushek/s400/Capture.JPG" alt="" id="BLOGGER_PHOTO_ID_5070736015703965586" border="0" /></a><br />Perhaps of more interest to the panoramic community as a whole is the ability to link in your own views using the recently released <a href="http://digitalurban.blogspot.com/2007/05/kml-22-panoramas-in-google-earth.html"><span>KML</span> 2.2 beta specifications</a>. This includes the ability to tile images, perhaps allowing fully <span>immersive</span>, high resolution <span>panoramas</span> in both Google Earth and Google Maps.<br /><br />You can <a href="http://www.google.com/maps?f=q&hl=en&amp;amp;amp;amp;amp;amp;amp;amp;q=94111&ie=UTF8&amp;om=0&layer=c&amp;cbll=37.793004,-122.402153&cbp=1,255.532807475884,0.50595288010623,0&ll=37.808089,-122.403488&spn=0.031872,0.058365&amp;amp;amp;amp;amp;amp;amp;z=14&gl=us">view the Street View direct from here</a>.</p>
