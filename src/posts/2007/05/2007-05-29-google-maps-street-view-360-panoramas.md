Available for Silicon Valley, Las Vegas, Miami, Denver and New York new areas will be added over the coming months. The YouTube movie below from Google explains the concept:<br /><br /><center><object width="425" height="350"><param name="movie" value="http://www.youtube.com/v/91wuBqlny50"></param><param name="wmode" value="transparent"></param><embed src="http://www.youtube.com/v/91wuBqlny50" type="application/x-shockwave-flash" wmode="transparent" width="425" height="350"></embed></object></center><br /><br />Captured using vehicle based panoramic cameras linked to a GPS the innovative nature of the panoramic views is the way in which the interface has been developed.
