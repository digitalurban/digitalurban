<p>We have updated our <a title="London Live Weather Data" href="http://www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/cumulus/ipad.html">live weather page</a> with a number of new features. The page updates every 3 seconds with a live feed from a Davis Vantage Pro 2 on the roof of 1-19 Torrington Place, just off Tottenham Court Road in London.<br />
<a href="http://www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/cumulus/ipad.html"><img class="wp-image-3074 aligncenter" style="border: 2px solid black;" title="London Weather Data" alt="London Weather Data" src="/assets/uploads/2013/03/Weatherdials-1.jpg" width="614" height="819" /></a><br />
The weather dials now include:<br />
<ul><br />
	<li>Visual Wind Rose - to show the average wind direction;</li><br />
	<li>Wind Run - an odometer style view of the total distance the wind has travelled in the last 24 hours;</li><br />
	<li>UV Index, showing the current UV level for London.</li><br />
</ul><br />
The page is optimised for a tablet/mobile view - <a title="Live London Weather Data" href="http://www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/cumulus/ipad.html">View the live London Weather Dials</a>.</p>
