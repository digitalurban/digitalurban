At the moment we are looking into ways to mount 6 iPads in the CASA office, home of digital urban. As part of our 'Analogies' grant we are looking at creating a portable exhibition space of urban research. Building on our recent iPad work on <a href="http://www.ucl.ac.uk/dh-blog/2011/03/16/animals-and-ipads-qrator-in-the-grant-museum/">QRator</a>, in association with <a href="http://www.ucl.ac.uk/dh/">Digital Humanities</a>, we are looking to create a conversation and twitter system linked to artefacts. However, in the case of Analogies, the artefacts are part of the urban system, looking at flows, networks, <a href="http://simulacra.blogs.casa.ucl.ac.uk/">simulacra and simulation</a>.<br /><br /><div style="clear: both; text-align: center;"><a href="https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/-mfUegSQzzMU/TYkVKIRZ53I/AAAAAAAACek/ubdX_mC_QJU/s1600/photo.JPG" style="margin-left: 1em; margin-right: 1em;"><img border="0" height="396" src="/assets/uploads/external/lh3.googleusercontent.com/-mfUegSQzzMU/TYkVKIRZ53I/AAAAAAAACek/ubdX_mC_QJU/s640/photo.JPG" width="640" /></a></div><br />Our first test has been using the <a href="http://www.thepadtab.com/">PadTab Tablet Mounting System</a> and we are impressed. Using a press on mount it is possible to slide an iPad on and off the wall. In a closed, monitored office space it is a quick and easy solution, the movie below provides more details:<br /><br /><br /><center><iframe allowfullscreen="" frameborder="0" height="390" src="http://www.youtube.com/embed/r8Lnr8MdHtA" title="YouTube video player" width="640"></iframe></center><br /><br />Security is of course an issue when you move to a more public space, but for a first look the PadTab is perfect for the office.
