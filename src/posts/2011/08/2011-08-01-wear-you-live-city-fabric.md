<p><span style="font-family: inherit;">We love 'Wear you Live' by <a href="http://www.cityfabric.net/">City Fabric</a> - in short its a series of products from T-Shirts to Tote Bags, Prints, Cushions printed with a figureground map.<span style="line-height: 22px;"> This type of map is commonly used in the fields of city planning, design and architecture to study density and development patterns by depicting all of the buildings in a city as opposed to the streets, parcels or transit lines</span> With 13 cities across the USA planned, the concept is currently raising funds via KickStarter:</span><br /><span style="font-family: inherit;"><br /></span><br /><span style="font-family: inherit;"></span><br /><br /><center><iframe frameborder="0" height="510px" src="http://www.kickstarter.com/projects/cityfabric/wear-you-live/widget/video.html" width="640px"></iframe></center><br />Its one of the those ideas where you sit back and think 'wow why didn't i think of that', we hope the project will roll out on a global basis soon as they raise funds, after all a London Tote Bag or Urban Cushion is surely essential.<br /><span style="font-family: inherit;"><br /></span><br /><span style="font-family: inherit;">Head over to the <a href="http://www.kickstarter.com/projects/cityfabric/wear-you-live">Wear you Live page on Kickstarter</a> to help them get going.</span></p>
