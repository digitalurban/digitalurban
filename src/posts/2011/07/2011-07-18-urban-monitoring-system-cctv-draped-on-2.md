<p><span style="font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;"><span style="line-height: 24px;"><span style="line-height: 16px;">An Urban Sensation is a proposal from Dan Foster-Smith, a graduate </span></span><span style="line-height: 16px;">from <a href="http://www.interaction.rca.ac.uk/course" style="background-attachment: initial; background-clip: initial; background-color: transparent; background-image: initial; background-origin: initial; border-bottom-width: 0px; border-color: initial; border-left-width: 0px; border-right-width: 0px; border-style: initial; border-top-width: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px; margin-left: 0px; margin-right: 0px; margin-top: 0px; outline-color: initial; outline-style: initial; outline-width: 0px; padding-bottom: 0px; padding-left: 0px; padding-right: 0px; padding-top: 0px; text-decoration: none; vertical-align: baseline;">Designing Interactions</a> at the Royal College of Arts</span><span style="line-height: 16px;">. It is aimed at developing an open source infrastructure that would enable the virtual reconstruction of our sensory stimuli at any location. The infrastructure enables our sense of sight, sound and smell to be emulated. For the sense of sight live CCTV footage is overlain on a 3D model of the urban environment. This allows you to move around the city space exploring visual soundings in real time. Combined with a GPS trace you can then re-live you visual surroundings or experience the visual surroundings of another person or just ponder what it would be like to be at hypothetical location:</span></span><br /><span style="font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;"><span style="line-height: 24px;"></span></span><br /><center><iframe frameborder="0" height="360" src="http://player.vimeo.com/video/25471417?title=0&byline=0&portrait=0&color=ffffff" width="640"></iframe></center><br /><span style="font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;"><span style="line-height: 24px;">We really like the use of CCTV to drape footage over a 3D model, it is well worth heading over to <a href="http://sensoryemulation.com/">http://sensoryemulation.com/</a> to find out more on the technique as well as <a href="http://danfs.co.uk/">Dans</a> own page for more projects.</span></span><br /></p>
