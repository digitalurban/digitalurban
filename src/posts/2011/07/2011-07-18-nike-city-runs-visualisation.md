<p><span style="line-height: 24px;"><span style="font-family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;">For the launch of the Nike Free Run+ 2 City Pack series, YesYesNo was invited to develop software that would allow runners to create dynamic paintings with their feet using their Nike+ GPS run data.
