<iframe src="http://www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/weather/dashboardcam.htm" width="100%" height="830px" align="left"></iframe> Weather data updates every 3 seconds via a Davis Vantage Pro located at University College London. The camera view streams a live feed looking back towards the weather station, timelapse movies are automated every 1 and 24 hours. The dashboard background colour changes according to temperature, updated every 5 degrees centigrade.
