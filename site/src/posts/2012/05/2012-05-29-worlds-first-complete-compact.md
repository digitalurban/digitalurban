At night or in bad weather, elite military and security professionals are reliant on technology to find potential threats. Infrared and video cameras can not get enough pixels on target to cover wide areas. But radar coupled with a PTZ camera and you have a winning solution that covers a wide area and which then allows you to slew the camera to the potential threat and identify the target.  <br /><br /><div style="clear: both; text-align: center;"><a href="http://1.bp.blogspot.com/-O38DxRwhcFE/T8T0Y6YrRGI/AAAAAAAAC04/nNccgHFD-Uc/s1600/17o0xtda4w3zxjpg.jpg" style="margin-left: 1em; margin-right: 1em;"><img border="0" height="225" src="/assets/uploads/external/1.bp.blogspot.com/-O38DxRwhcFE/T8T0Y6YrRGI/AAAAAAAAC04/nNccgHFD-Uc/s400/17o0xtda4w3zxjpg.jpg" width="400" /></a></div><br />Now thats all well and good in a slightly over the top military manner but in the centre of a city there is not much need to use a personal elite military system.
