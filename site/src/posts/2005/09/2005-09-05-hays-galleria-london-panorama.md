<p><img src="/assets/uploads/external/www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/andy/blogimages/Hays.jpg" /><br /><br />Hays Galleria is tucked along side the river between London and Tower Bridge. A mix of small boutiques, coffee shops, bars and restaurants it is a place to browser and relax as part of a river side walk in London.<br /><br />The galleria is spanned by a steel and glass roof which covers the original Hay's Wharf - built in the 1850's and a centre point for river trade in London.<br /><br />David Kemp's sculpture 'Navigators' dominates the centre, a nautical sculpture which captures the wharfs link to the river.<br /><br /><a href="http://www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/andy/qt/Hays.html">View the Quicktime panorama of Hays Galleria, London </a>(3.2mb).</p>
