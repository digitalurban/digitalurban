<p><img src="/assets/uploads/external/www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/andy/blogimages/Towerb.jpg" /><br /><br />Tower Bridge was completed in 1894, after 8 years in construction. The bridge was built to take pressure of London Bridge as the area to its east grew in population. <br /><br />Built to compliment the Tower of London's  gothic architecture, the bridge's unique design the result of a collaboration between Horace Jones, the then City Architect and John Wolfe Barry.<br /><br />Tower Bridge's colours of red, white and blue are a recent addition, added in 1977 to celebrate the Queen's Silver Jubilee. Previous to the Jubilee the bridge was painted brown.<br /><br /><br /><a href="http://www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/andy/qt/Towerb.html">View the Quicktime panorama of Tower Bridge, London </a>(1.8mb).</p>
