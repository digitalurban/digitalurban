<p><img src="/assets/uploads/external/www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/andy/blogimages/Tavistock.jpg" /><br /><br />Tavistock Square contains a small shaded park in the Bloomsbury sector of London. The panorama is taken at the central feature of the park - a statue of Mahatma Gandhi. The statue has been in the park since 1968 and was designed by Fredda Brilliant. <br /><br />The park also contains trees planted to commemorate the victims of the Hiroshima bombing and a Conscientious Objectors' memorial. <br /><br />It is a quiet place to sit and the panorama displays a distinct contrast to the scene in July 2005 when a bus was blown up on the square just a few metres from the park.<br /><br /><a href="http://www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/andy/qt/Tavistock.html">View the Quicktime panorama of Tavistock Square, London </a>(3.2mb).</p>
