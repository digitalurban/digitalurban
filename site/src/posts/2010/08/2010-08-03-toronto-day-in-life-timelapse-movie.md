The timelapse by Robert Fisher entitled 'A day in the life of Toronto' consists of 18 hours of real time compressed to just over 3 minutes.
The movie documents the typical goings on of daily life in Toronto, from the business rush of King &amp; Bay to tourists along the waterfront and at CN Tower to shopping in Chinatown to a human statue street performer to simply waiting for the TTC. The city of Toronto, its people and places are the lead performers:
