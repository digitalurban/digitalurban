We have given ourselves a suitable talking to for missing blog posts and now we are back...<br /><br />First up is the movie above, which explains some of our downtime. At CASA we are using Second Life as a online urban laboratory to explore issues pertaining to urban planning and public debate in a visually 3D collaborative environment.<br /><br />We have created three agent-based models ranging from Conways Game of Life, Schelling's segregation model and a pedestrian evacuation model. The models are proofs of concept to demonstrate how experts, model builders and the non specialist can view, interact and discuss agent-based models within Second Life.<br /><br />The music was not our choice :)  take a look at <a href="http://www.gisagents.blogspot.com">gisagents.blogspot.com</a> for the full story and more of a background on agents in Second Life.</p>
