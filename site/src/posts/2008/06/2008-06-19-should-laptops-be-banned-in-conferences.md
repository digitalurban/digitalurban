<p><a href="http://www.wasso.co.jp/blog/image/mac%20book.jpg"><img style="margin: 0px auto 10px; display: block; text-align: center; cursor: pointer; width: 400px;" src="http://www.wasso.co.jp/blog/image/mac%20book.jpg" alt="" border="0" /></a>We are at the 4<span>th</span> International Conference on e-Social Science listening to a talk by Alex <span>Voss</span> entitled 'Widening Uptake of e-Infrastructure Services'. The worrying thing is that the majority of the people in the room are using their laptops to check email/surf the web/write blog posts (like us) rather than listen to the presentation.<br /><br />This poses the questions <span>whether</span> laptops should be banned from such conferences during presentations? It would mean less blog posts but as a presenter myself it can be disheartening to look up only to view a sea of laptops and people typing.<br /><br /><span>Ok</span> time to stop typing and to listen....</p>
