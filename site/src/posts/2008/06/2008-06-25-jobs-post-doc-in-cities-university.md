<p><a href="http://www.ucd.ie/gpep/gpepinfo/images/gpepbanner2.gif"><img style="margin: 0px auto 10px; display: block; text-align: center; cursor: pointer; width: 400px;" src="http://www.ucd.ie/gpep/gpepinfo/images/gpepbanner2.gif" alt="" border="0" /></a><a href="http://www.ucd.ie/gpep/gpepinfo/index.html">The School of Geography, Planning and Environmental Policy</a> is advertising a Post-Doctoral research position looking at cities.<br /><br />The research project, which is a EU 6th framework project, explores city and regional competitiveness with a particular focus on the idea of the creative class and the creative city.<br /><br />Candidates should have a PhD (or be near to completion of a PhD) in urban/economic/regional geography or in a related subject. For further information about the post please contact Dr. Declan Redmond at declan.redmond@ucd.ie<br /><br />The post is funded by the European Commission and is for two years, starting on September 1st 2008. Salary is approximately €38,000 to €45,000 per annum depending on qualifications and experience.<br /><br />To apply for this position please forward (by e-mail) a letter of application and a Curriculum Vitae (naming 2 referees) to Dr. Declan Redmond by July 16th, 2008.<br /><br />See<a href="http://www2.fmg.uva.nl/acre/"> http://www2.fmg.uva.nl/acre/</a> for more information on the research area.</p>
