<p><a href="http://1.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/R6mJKSN8iwI/AAAAAAAABDU/MTUHXz5pm54/s1600-h/Lprofile.jpg"><img style="display:block; margin:0px auto 10px; text-align:center;cursor:pointer; cursor:hand;" src="/assets/uploads/external/1.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/R6mJKSN8iwI/AAAAAAAABDU/MTUHXz5pm54/s400/Lprofile.jpg" border="0" alt=""id="BLOGGER_PHOTO_ID_5163809257539013378" /></a><br />London Profiler is a <a href="http://www.londonprofiler.org/">web site</a> that enables users to build up a picture of the geo-demographics of Greater London from data on population attributes such as cultural/ethnicity, deprivation, the extent of e-literacy, level of higher education, and health related problems together with the synthetic free geodemographic classifications available in the UK.<br /><br />The site allows users to visualise themes at different scales, to search by postcode or zoom at borough level, to change the layer's transparency and to add KML layers.<br /><br />Developed by our lab (<a href="http://www.casa.ucl.ac.uk">CASA</a>) we are slightly biased here but we think its a great use of Google Maps combined with more traditional GIS data that would not normally be available online in such an easy to use interface.<br /><br />We will have more on the concept in the coming weeks with the launch of a new mapping website, can't say too much at the moment but more news soon as we can...<br /><br />Take the London Profiler for a spin at <a href="http://www.londonprofiler.org/">http://www.londonprofiler.org/</a></p>
