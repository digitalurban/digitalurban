Its not a negative view of the world by any means, in many ways its a celebration of what is a bit grim in our urban environment - maybe its a London thing to appreciate the urban space around us that is slightly rubbish?<br /><br />We will be featuring some of the recent contributions in the coming weeks - thanks to all those who have contributed so far and keep adding them to our <a href="http://www.flickr.com/groups/608622@N22/">Flick Pool</a> :)<br /><br />Andy</p>
