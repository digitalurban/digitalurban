The music video above entitled 'Nice Clean White' is an abstract journey in our everyday surroundings, illustrating moments of life in a continuous way.<br /><br />The clip contains 4500 still photographs  where urban landscapes meet pastoral scenes to create a whirlpool around the theme of the video: the subjective point of view and urban life.<br /><br />Created with a stop motion technique, it was conceived with a very simple Nikon <span>Coolpix</span> E4600. The clip is video free, created entirely from still photos taken in the streets of Nancy and <span>Epinal</span> in France.<br /><br />See the main video page on <span>Vimeo</span> by <a href="http://www.vimeo.com/dabrainkilla"><span>DaBrainkilla</span></a> for more info.</p>
