Perhaps more interesting here however, to look beyond the visuals for once, is the audio accompanying project. Below is a description in the words of the company behind the soundscape, <a href="http://www.vimeo.com/user666794">Radium Audio Ltd</a>:<br /><br />Project: Saxton - Leeds - Green Spaces<br />Developer: Urban Splash - United Kingdom<br />Production Company: The Neighbourhood<br />Director: The Neighbourhood<br /><br />Background: Urban Splash is a property developer in the UK and commissioned The Neighbourhood to complete 6 short animated films.<br /><br />How we did it: Our task was to create the music and sound design for all 6 films. The first film in the series is Green Spaces. We created a wonderful bubbly guitar score that fits the visual mood perfectly, summer time, fun, barbeques, and carefree days. The sound design score reflects the visual element, and also adds a sparkle to the events.<br /><br />Sounds we created include: Music score, buzzing bees, rainbow texture, camera movement swooshes, foley, atmosphere track, people & animal track.<br /><br />-<br /><br />Sound can be easily overlooked, yet it perhaps one of the most important elements of an architectural animation - see <a href="http://www.vimeo.com/user666794">Radium Audio Ltd's page on Vimeo </a>for more examples.</p>
