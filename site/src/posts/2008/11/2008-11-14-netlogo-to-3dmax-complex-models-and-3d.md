<p>We have been running a series of posts recently looking at crowd simulation, dynamics and particles in 3D Max. The aim is to visualise the complex systems that make up the city within an environment that allows both clear and easy visualisation and export capabilities to other packages such as Crysis or Google Earth.<br /><br />While 3D Max is of use for crowd and particle simulation when it comes to modelling complex systems an external package is required, such as <a href="http://ccl.northwestern.edu/netlogo/">NetLogo</a>. The movie below details our first steps at CASA (home of digital urban) to export a basic traffic model from NetLogo into 3D Studio Max. It is particularly well suited for modeling complex systems developing over time. Modelers can give instructions to hundreds or thousands of independent "agents" all operating concurrently. This makes it possible to explore the connection between the micro-level behavior of individuals and the macro-level patterns that emerge from the interaction of many individuals.(<a href="http://www.nationmaster.com/encyclopedia/NetLogo">Nation Master Encyclopedia</a>)<br /><br />We will have more posts on complex systems and visualising the city over the coming weeks and months.<br /><br />See <a href="http://gisagents.blogspot.com/">http://gisagents.blogspot.com</a>/ for more on modelling, agents and the city.</p>
