<p><span>MapTube</span> has just entered a new beta phase allowing both <span>kml</span> and <span>kmz</span> files to be linked in.<br /><br /><a href="http://www.maptube.org/"><img style="margin: 0px auto 10px; display: block; text-align: center; cursor: pointer;" src="/assets/uploads/external/3.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/SIidr14ZxNI/AAAAAAAABRI/8H4fpJRuesI/s400/maptubekml.png" alt="" id="BLOGGER_PHOTO_ID_5226600744087045330" border="0" /></a>Adding <span>kml</span> into the mix allows any map created via <span>MyMaps</span> to be viewed on <span>MapTube</span> and layered with any other map on the system. Its in beta but linking is easy, simply register and then rather than point us to your .<span>xml</span> file, simply point us to any .<span>kml</span> or <span>kmz</span>. Add in a description and click save, the map is now part of <span>MapTube</span> and ready to be viewed alongside any of the other converted .<span>shp</span>, .<span>csv</span>, <span>kml</span> or <span>kmz</span> files.<br /><br /><a href="http://www.maptube.org/"><img style="margin: 0px auto 10px; display: block; text-align: center; cursor: pointer;" src="/assets/uploads/external/3.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/SIigRo62xxI/AAAAAAAABRQ/UUsnlp_QCsg/s400/MapTubekml2.png" alt="" id="BLOGGER_PHOTO_ID_5226603592465958674" border="0" /></a><br /><a href="http://www.maptube.org/">http://www.maptube.org</a> - select 'latest uploads' and our first test <span>kml</span> of the Top 25 Web 2.0 Companies Locations should be viewable, the file is linked in direct from Google <span>MyMaps</span>. The ability to pull in <span>kml</span> form other sites, such as <span>MyMaps</span> allows for an interesting mix and match of both of <span>neo</span> and professional geography side by side.<br /><br />Note <span>MapTube</span> comes out of <a href="http://www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/"><span>CASA</span></a>, the home of digital urban. Software to convert <span>ESRI</span> .<span>shp</span> files to Google Maps and <span>MapTube</span> is available free of charge via the <span>MapTube</span> site once you register, registration is free.<br /><br />We will have more examples and updates over the coming weeks.</p>
