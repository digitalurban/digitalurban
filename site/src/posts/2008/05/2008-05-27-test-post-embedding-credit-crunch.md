<p><a href="http://3.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/SDbQ4nbMWFI/AAAAAAAABME/ccA6fzkxARA/s320/Map1.png"><img style="margin: 0pt 0pt 10px 10px; float: right; cursor: pointer; width: 320px;" src="/assets/uploads/external/3.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/SDbQ4nbMWFI/AAAAAAAABME/ccA6fzkxARA/s320/Map1.png" alt="" border="0" /></a>Maps represent a moment in time and when your creating a mood map of the United Kingdom. In relation to the countries on the impact of the Credit Crunch, 24 hours can be a long time, as we explore below.<br /><br />Our work with BBC Radio 4's PM show is yielding interesting results with the fuel prices coming out as the nations biggest concern in these times of tight credit. Yet look closer at the data and it becomes clear that the nations mood has shifted over the last few weeks.<br /><br />To recap, listener's are being asked to answer the following question:<br /><br /><span style="font-weight: bold;">What single factor is hurting you most about the credit crunch?</span><br /><span style="font-weight: bold;"><br />Please select one of the answers below:<br /></span><br /><span style="font-weight: bold;">Mortgage or Rent</span><br /><span style="font-weight: bold;">Fuel<br />Food Prices<br />Holidays<br />Other<br />The Credit Crunch is not affecting me</span><br /><br />We generated a new map every 30 minutes, providing an up to date view of the nations mood.<br /><br />In addition to mapping the data it can also be analysed over time, the image below represents the percentage of answers on May 1st:<br /><a href="http://3.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/SDwuGHbMWJI/AAAAAAAABMs/Idnsp9oQyMM/s1600-h/May1st.png"><img style="margin: 0px auto 10px; display: block; text-align: center; cursor: pointer;" src="/assets/uploads/external/3.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/SDwuGHbMWJI/AAAAAAAABMs/Idnsp9oQyMM/s400/May1st.png" alt="" id="BLOGGER_PHOTO_ID_5205085951940647058" border="0" /></a>On May 1st the nations biggest concern was the price of food, yet by May 3rd fuel had taken over with food slipping back down in terms of overall percentage:<br /><a href="http://1.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/SDwuWnbMWKI/AAAAAAAABM0/k1Qh3f9h0jA/s1600-h/May3rd.png"><img style="margin: 0px auto 10px; display: block; text-align: center; cursor: pointer;" src="/assets/uploads/external/1.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/SDwuWnbMWKI/AAAAAAAABM0/k1Qh3f9h0jA/s400/May3rd.png" alt="" id="BLOGGER_PHOTO_ID_5205086235408488610" border="0" /></a>The nations mood over the last few weeks has been changing quite notably over time, perhaps in relations to the amount of media coverage anyone topic has received. Food prices were in the news earlier in the month and now fuel prices are hitting the headlines with the current blockage of London by lorry drivers.<br /><br />At the present time fuel prices are again leading the poll, you can view the timeline using the interactive version embedded below.
