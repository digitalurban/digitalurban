<br /><span style="font-family: arial, sans-serif;"><span style="border-collapse: collapse; font-size: 14px;">A early heads up that the trailer for the forthcoming podcast from </span></span><a href="http://www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/" style="border-collapse: collapse; font-family: arial, sans-serif; font-size: 14px;">CASA</a><span style="font-family: arial, sans-serif;"><span style="border-collapse: collapse; font-size: 14px;"> - The Global Lab is now available on iTunes. </span></span><span style="border-collapse: collapse; font-family: arial, sans-serif; font-size: 14px;">Global Lab is a podcast about cities, global connectivity and the impact of technology produced here at UCL's Centre for Advanced Spatial Analysis.
