<p>Here in CASA, University College London, we are currently waiting for the final part of our home made <a href="http://reprap.org/wiki/Main_Page">RepRap 3D Printer</a>. Unfortunately it is a crucial part - the hot head that melts the plastic, allowing the printer to lay down layers of material and create objects.<br /><br />While we are waiting, <a href="http://www.stevenjamesgray.com/">Steven Gray</a> has modified the RepRap and replaced the hot head with a marker pen. This simple swap is actually an interesting concept, allowing the printer to draw:<br /><br /><iframe allowfullscreen="" frameborder="0" height="390" src="http://www.youtube.com/embed/lhPSJ7TTg7c" title="YouTube video player" width="640"></iframe><br /><br />In many ways its a high tech automated Etch A Sketch and for that we love it...</p>
