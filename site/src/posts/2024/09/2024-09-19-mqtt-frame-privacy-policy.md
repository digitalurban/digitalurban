<p><strong>Effective Date:</strong> [19th March 2025]</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how MQTTFrame ("we," "our," or "us") handles your information.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Information We Collect</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>MQTTFrame does not collect, store, or share any personal data or user information. The app is designed solely to display real-time data through user-configured MQTT topics. All MQTT data subscriptions are entered and controlled by you, the user, and remain exclusively on your device.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">How We Use Information</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>MQTTFrame does not collect or transmit any user data or personal information. Data configured and displayed by the app remains locally stored on your device.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Data Storage and Security</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>MQTTFrame stores your MQTT configuration settings (such as MQTT broker details and subscribed topics) locally on your device. We do not have access to this information, and it is never transmitted externally.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Third-Party Services</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>MQTTFrame does not utilize third-party analytics services or third-party advertising.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Changes to This Policy</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We may occasionally update our Privacy Policy. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to periodically review this Privacy Policy for the latest information on our privacy practices.</p>
