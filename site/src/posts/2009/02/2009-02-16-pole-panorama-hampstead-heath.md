Boudicca's Mound is a tumulus where, according to local legend, Queen Boudicea was buried after she and 10,000 other Iceni soldiers were defeated at Battle Bridge.<br /><br />The aim of the panorama was to capture the use of the 30 foot panoramic pole by way of a tutorial for the blog.
