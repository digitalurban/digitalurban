What would happen when these courtyards become a part of the urban space?<br /><br />The Parallel City is an invitation to rediscover and rethink the historic city of Copenhagen - you can find out more and full details at: <a href="http://theparallelcity.blogspot.com/">http://theparallelcity.blogspot.com/</a> - its a nice idea, well presented thus the post :)
