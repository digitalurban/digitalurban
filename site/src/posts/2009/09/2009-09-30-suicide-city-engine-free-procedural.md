<p>You wait for years for a free building/city generator and then two come along in the space of the week. Yesterday we detailed the <a href="http://digitalurban.blogspot.com/2009/09/free-procedural-buildings-generator-now.html">Procedural Building Generator</a> for Max and now the Suicidator City Engine (SCE) has been released. Running via a Python script in Blender, the results are as equally impressive:<br /><br /><center><object width="600" height="345"><param name="movie" value="http://www.youtube.com/v/HROU3gLcgfg&hl=en&fs=1&"></param><param name="allowFullScreen" value="true"></param><param name="allowscriptaccess" value="always"></param><embed src="http://www.youtube.com/v/HROU3gLcgfg&hl=en&fs=1&" type="application/x-shockwave-flash" allowscriptaccess="always" allowfullscreen="true" width="600" height="345"></embed></object></center><br /><br />The script allows you to automatically create a city by adjusting various parameters, such as city size and complexity, rather than creating each building, each street, and each texture manually. <br /><br />Creating detailed cities and buildings has suddenly got so much easier and indeed free, which can only be a good thing.<br /><br />Head over to the <a href="http://arnaud.ile.nc/sce/index.php">Suicidator City Engine page</a> for full details.</p>
