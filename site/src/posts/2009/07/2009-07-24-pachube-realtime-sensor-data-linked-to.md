<div>Pachube is a web service available at <a href="http://www.pachube.com/" title="http://www.pachube.com">http://www.pachube.com</a> that enables you to connect, tag and share real time sensor data from objects, devices, buildings and environments around the world.<br /><br />The key aim is to facilitate interaction between remote environments, both physical and virtual. If you have not heard of Pachube before it is well worth taking a look at their site and the exploring the concept which is a little bit like YouTube, except that, rather than sharing videos, Pachube enables people to monitor and share real time environmental data from sensors that are connected to the internet.<br /><br />The linkages can now be visualised via SketchUp, the incoming data can be used to generate or modulate a 3D model of a building or environment, and enables sophisticated design-decisions that are based on actual (and not simulated) sensor and environment data. Possible uses include designing interactive façades, undertaking post-occupancy evaluation (which is where you evaluate how well the design performs) and sensor-based form generation:<br /><br /><center><object height="345" width="600"><param name="movie" value="http://www.youtube.com/v/QhzA5_oFKuA&hl=en&fs=1&"><param name="allowFullScreen" value="true"><param name="allowscriptaccess" value="always"><embed src="http://www.youtube.com/v/QhzA5_oFKuA&hl=en&fs=1&" type="application/x-shockwave-flash" allowscriptaccess="always" allowfullscreen="true" height="345" width="600"></embed></object></center><br /><br />The demo details real-time water, gas and energy usage from a building management system being graphed on top of a model of the building. Although in this demo the data is merely driving simple scalar transformations to visualize energy usage, the real potential of the plug-in is to serve <a href="http://www.pachube.com/">Pachube</a> data to real-world design tools to directly inform design decisions in a much more sophisticated way.<br /><br /></div><div>Download the plug-in and see the tutorial here: <a href="http://community.pachube.com/sketchup">http://community.pachube.com/sketchup</a><br /><br />Picked up via <a href="http://www.urbantick.blogspot.com/">UrbanTick</a></div>
