We like the construction drawings section and its an interesting insight into the use of SketchUp by an architect - we know its a controversial subject with some architects loving SketchUp and some viewing it as simplistic.<br /><br />Our view? Some people we have met on our travels take a rather 'elitist' view of SketchUp, we think it has been probably one of the most important steps forward in 3D software to date, simply for its ease of use, its mass market availability and while being simple still allowing complex work flows and intricate model creation.<br /><br />Discuss...</p>
