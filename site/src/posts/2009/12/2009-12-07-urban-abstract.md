Picked up via <a href="http://urbantick.blogspot.com/">UrbanTick</a> - Art Director Jopsu Ramu from Musuta Ltd. (a multidisciplinary design  agency based in Helsinki & Tokyo) has created together with Shun  Kawakami (artless Inc) an artist and designer from Tokyo - a digital art piece  titled Urban Abstract.
