<p>The movie below is our first timelapse test using the Canon G9, a power supply and the free CHDK firmware 'addon' for a range of Canon cameras.<br /><br /><object height="345" width="600"><param name="movie" value="http://www.youtube.com/v/0M8bNX4jzt8&hl=en_US&fs=1&"><param name="allowFullScreen" value="true"><param name="allowscriptaccess" value="always"><embed src="http://www.youtube.com/v/0M8bNX4jzt8&hl=en_US&fs=1&" type="application/x-shockwave-flash" allowscriptaccess="always" allowfullscreen="true" height="345" width="600"></embed></object><br /><br />Music <a href="http://www.mp3unsigned.com/showmp3.asp?mp3ID=56263">'City Lights' by Ox on MP3 Unsigned</a>.<br /><br />As mentioned, movie was created by capturing a 5 mega pixel image every 5 seconds, this allowed enough resolution to do the pan in post processing as well as the ability to crop out sections of the image.<br /><br />As long as we got the settings correct you should be able to run the clip at 1920x1080 via YouTube. <a href="http://chdk.wikia.com/wiki/CHDK">CHDK </a>allows a wide range of Canon cameras to be used as timelapse rigs - indeed it arguably makes Canon the first choice for anyone wishing to creating an HD timelapse.<br /><br />The movie is our first test, the technique should allow some good 'stacked night time images' of the city to be created - the image below was created from 8000 images to reveal activity in the sky above a city at night:<br /><br /><a href="http://www.flickr.com/photos/84334252@N00/3065726752/" title="8401 Images Stacked: London Sky at Night by DigitalUrban, on Flickr"><img src="/assets/uploads/external/farm4.static.flickr.com/3010/3065726752_a682d1e2af_o.jpg" alt="8401 Images Stacked: London Sky at Night" height="490" width="600" /></a><br /><br />The image was created using a simple webcam -<a href="http://digitalurban.blogspot.com/2008/11/tutorial-image-stacking-for-star-trails.html"> see our tutorial for more details</a>, with CHDK we should be able to enhance this considerably.</p>
