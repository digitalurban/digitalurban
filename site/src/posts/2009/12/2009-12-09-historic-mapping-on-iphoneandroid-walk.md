<p>Walking Through Time is a research project developing iPhone and Android apps that architectural historians, conservationists and tourists to swap the Google base map for a historical map - i.e., allowing a user to 'Walk Through Time'.<br /><br />The movie clip below provides a neat guide to the concept:<br /><br /><center><object height="345" width="600"><param name="movie" value="http://www.youtube.com/v/bJwYv-6wgf8&hl=en_US&fs=1&"><param name="allowFullScreen" value="true"><param name="allowscriptaccess" value="always"><embed src="http://www.youtube.com/v/bJwYv-6wgf8&hl=en_US&fs=1&" type="application/x-shockwave-flash" allowscriptaccess="always" allowfullscreen="true" height="345" width="600"></embed></object></center><br /><br />In short, we really like this, the ability to load up a historical map based on your current location opens up all sort of possibilities from historical tours through to  viewing the changing layout of the city over time.<br /><br />The project is funded through a JISC grant and is collaboration between Edinburgh College of Art (ECA) and Edinburgh University (UoE) with development led by <a href="http://x2.i-dat.org/~cs/works.html">Chris Speed</a> and <a href="http://www.eca.ac.uk/index.php?id=643">Ian Campbell</a> of ECA with Tim Urwin, EDINA / UoE, Petra Leimlehner, UoE,Peter Pratt, UoE, Karlyn Sutherland, UoE.<br /><br />The app is currently in prototype form  - keep an eye on <a href="http://walkingthroughtime.eca.ac.uk/">http://walkingthroughtime.eca.ac.uk/</a>  for more news on a release.</p>
