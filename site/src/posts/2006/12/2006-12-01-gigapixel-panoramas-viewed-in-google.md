By using a zoom lens and stitching software you can capture in scene in extremely high resolution -  typically in excess of 95,000 by 40,000 pixels using a 300mm lens and 612 overlapping images.<br /><br />If you then take the technology behind Google Maps and replace the maps with the resulting image you get a plugin free navigation tool.<br /><br />The <a href="http://www.xrez.com/">XRez site</a> is well worth a visit...</p>
