We are posting this as a reader requested a high resolution copy of the movie that accompanies our <a href="http://digitalurban.blogspot.com/2006/06/second-lifes-digital-metaverse.html">original Second Life Post</a>.<br /><br />Sadly that now only exists on YouTube due to a mix up with our Recycle Bin. Back in the days when <a href="http://www.activeworlds.com">ActiveWorlds</a> was the next big thing and we ran the <a href="http://digitalurban.blogspot.com/2005/05/avatar-teleports-30-days-in.html">30 Days in ActiveWorlds</a> experiment we bet our bottom dollar that Virtual Worlds were about to become main stream. They didn't of course and a number of companies went bankrupt.  Second Life it seems is giving the Virtual World a second chance.</p>
