<p><img src="/assets/uploads/external/www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/andy/blogimages/twinmovie.jpg" /><br /><br />Every now and again innovations come along that make us smile. This week has seen two come our attention (more on the second in later posts). The first one is the ability to embed YouTube movies into panoramas creating the ability to pump out adverts or just general content in panoramic space. <br /><br />Using <a href="http://www.devalvr.com/index.html">DevalVR</a> which is the best new viewer on the block, it is possible to stream in movies. There are various examples out there using whiteboards or plasma tv's in panoramas but if your going to do an example its best to do it big and show a movie over Twin Peaks San Francisco.<br /><br />Load up the example, pan round to the Twin Peaks Transmitters and click play... its our Ipod and Urban Perception movie looking out towards San Fran.<br /><br /><a href="http://www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/andy/qt/twinmovie.html">View the Panorama of Twin Peaks with the Embedded Movie</a></p>
