<p>Back to work after last weeks <span>AGI</span> conference and this week brings a range of thoughts on creating cities and visualising urban form.<br /><br />We will be posting some more Google Earth Panoramas shortly as well as a quick and easy way to create clouds in <span>Photoshop</span>. Also later today, if we get the time, there will be a post on <span>PixelArt</span> and cities, an <span>intriguing</span> way to draw and visualise urban form in <span>MSPaint</span>.<br /><br />Later this week we are at a conference on Grid Computing to look at batch rendering for city visualisation and simple ways to visualise data in Google Maps. At <span>CASA</span> we have produced a demo of visualising GPS data in both Google Maps and Google Earth <span>simultaneously</span> complete with animation. We should be able to get the demo up later this week.<br /><br />Finally a free piece of software to convert <span>ESRI</span> .<span>shp</span> files to Google Maps is in the pipeline from CASA and we will run a preview on this as soon as we can.</p>
