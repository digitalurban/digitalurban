<p><img src="/assets/uploads/external/www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/andy/blogimages/fantasylondon1.jpg" /><br /><br />While we are still sorting out the license for real London data we thought it would be interesting to explore the concept of a previous post <a href="http://digitalurban.blogspot.com/2005/11/imaginary-cities-fantasy-architecture.html">'Fantasy Architecture'</a> in London. <br /><br />We have used a sample file, created using gratuitous use of a Greeble Plugin, to illustrate the concept.<br /><br />If there is a building you would like to see built in London then email us either a 3d file or even a drawing of a skyscraper that you would like to see. We will then model it and place it online, creating a database of fantasy architecture in London.<br /><br />We look forward to your drawings - they can be as simple as you like..<br /><br /><img src="/assets/uploads/external/www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/andy/blogimages/fantasylondon2.jpg" /><br /><br />You can download the kmz file to fly into the model in Google Earth <a href="http://www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/andy/kmz/FantasyLondon1.kmz">here</a> (300k)<br /><br />To view the building in context you can download the kmz file of Natwest/Swiss Re and St Helens <a href="http://www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/andy/kmz/Natwestandswissandhelens.kmz">here</a> (35k)</p>
