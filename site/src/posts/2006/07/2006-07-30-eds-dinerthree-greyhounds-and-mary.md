<p><img src="/assets/uploads/external/www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/andy/blogimages/EdsDiner.jpg" /><br /><br />Ed's Diner is located in Soho in London. Based on the Apple Pan Diner in Los Angeles is was established in 1987 and features a retro 1950's decor. <br /><br />Panning around the scene you will see the Three Greyhounds Pub in Greek Street. Featuring a mock Tudor exterior which has been recently restored.<br /><br />Finally of note is the Cameron Mackintosh production of <a href="http://www.marypoppinsthemusical.co.uk/homepage.php">Mary Poppins </a>at the Prince Edward Theatre in Old Crompton Street. <a href="http://www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/andy/qt/EdsDiner.html">View the panorama of Ed's Diner/The Three Greyhounds and Mary Poppins, London </a>(2.9Mb).</p>
