One of the questions in modelling a 'living city' is how to combine aspects of agent based modelling for crowds, traffic simulation and 3D cities without a work flow that takes weeks and a high end graphics house.

Using the <a href="http://rodrigopegorari.net/blog/?p=30">intriguing P5 to Max script</a> it is possible to control 3D Max via Processing and a local server connection, this opens up a raft of possibilities for visualisation and urban scenes based on real data.
