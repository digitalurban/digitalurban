In a bus based game the pace is slower so it is easier to view how the city was constructed.<br /><br />If they were to make a London version they would have to include a 'hoodie' with his/her mobile phone on 'speaker' mode playing 'Urbanz Diss' on the 168 to Camden Town.</p>
