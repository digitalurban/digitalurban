<p><a href="http://1.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/RvVIy4E2lvI/AAAAAAAAAqY/6xVxFJKXjSw/s1600-h/9-5-07-official_ipod_touch.jpg"><img style="margin: 0pt 0pt 10px 10px; float: right; cursor: pointer;" src="/assets/uploads/external/1.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/RvVIy4E2lvI/AAAAAAAAAqY/6xVxFJKXjSw/s320/9-5-07-official_ipod_touch.jpg" alt="" id="BLOGGER_PHOTO_ID_5113072990832465650" border="0" /></a>In essence the only plus points about the <span>IPod</span> Touch are the extra memory (if you go for the 16<span>Gb</span> option) and not having to sign up to a new phone contract, compared to the IPhone.<br /><br />The minus points are many with various niggles about lack of a volume control on the unit meaning having to remove the <span>Ipod</span> from your pocket and touch twice/swipe <span>everytime</span> you want to skip a track or change the volume. <a href="http://www.engadget.com/2007/09/22/ipod-touch-review/"><span>Engadget</span></a> have just put up <a href="http://www.engadget.com/2007/09/22/ipod-touch-review/">their thoughts</a> and we have to say that we agree with most of their views.<br /><br />In addition to these points are the lack of applications compared to the IPhone, notably in our view the Google Maps App. However, as the IPhone and the <span>IPod</span> Touch run the same operating system the apps seem to be <span>interchangeable</span> as long as you can find a way to transfer them.<br /><br />We <span>don't</span> recommend this due to issues of <span>warranty</span> and copyright but <a href="http://www.hacktheipodtouch.com/install-application/maps/google-maps-app-on-ipod-touch"><span>HackTheIpodTouch</span>.com are reporting</a> the ability to transfer Maps.app to the <span>Ipod</span> Touch. No word on levels of success yet but if you want to fill up your <span>Ipod</span> Touch screen with all those missing apps we are betting the chance is only a few weeks away.<br /><br />With it of course comes a void <span>warranty</span> and risk of legal action etc etc....<br /><br />For those interested our <span>Ipod</span> Touch is going back to the Apple Store in Regent Street, London, next week as its screen is shockingly bad for playing video, see <a href="http://www.apple-touch.com/ipod-touch-screen-problem-negative-black-effect-1534.php">Apple Touch for more info</a>.</p>
