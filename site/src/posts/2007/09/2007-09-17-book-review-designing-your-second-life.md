It provides an almost essential guide to the ins and outs of avatar creation and perhaps more importantly architectural construction in the virtual environment.<br /><br />Starting out in Second Life can be confusing in terms of learning how to claim land, place your first objects and learning how to use textures. Rebecca provides a step by step walk through of the process starting off with a basic house through to full landscaping and a castle.<br /><br />If you are new to Second Life or looking to start building or buying land then Designing Your Second Life is the perfect accompaniment.<br /><br />You can <a href="http://astore.amazon.com/digiurba-20/detail/0321503015/105-4960733-4576464">buy Designing Your Second Life from our Amazon Store</a> at the discounted price of $19.99.<br /><br />See also <a href="http://digitalurban.blogspot.com/2007/03/book-of-week-second-life-official-guide.html">Second Life - The Official Guide</a>.<br /><br />Note a UK friendly store is coming soon...</p>
