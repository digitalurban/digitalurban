<p>"Remind Me"/"So Easy" was the first single from the <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norway" title="Norway">Norwegian</a> duo <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Röyksopp" title="Röyksopp">Röyksopp</a>'s debut album <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melody_A.M." title="Melody A.M.">Melody A.M.</a> The song features a visually stunning animated video, directed by the French motion graphics studio <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H5" title="H5">H5</a>.<br /><br />It features a day in the life of a woman working in the London's Square Mile solely through <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infographic" title="Infographic">infographics</a>; this includes labelled close-ups of everyday objects, product lifecycles, schematic diagrams, charts, and is generally illustrated in a simple isometric visual style.<br /><br />Of note is the city based visualisation interweaved with general geographic and data landscapes:<br /><br /><object height="355" width="425"><param name="movie" value="http://www.youtube.com/v/Q-v8WHF4XEI&rel=1"><param name="wmode" value="transparent"><embed src="http://www.youtube.com/v/Q-v8WHF4XEI&rel=1" type="application/x-shockwave-flash" wmode="transparent" height="355" width="425"></embed></object><br /><br />More information on Royksopp can be found at <a href="http://www.royksopp.com/">http://www.royksopp.com/</a></p>
