There are many solutions to the problem of creating digital cities, central to them all though is the need to tag information geographically to the 3d buildings. For this you need a GIS system, only then can the model be of direct use above and beyond the traditional CAD usage.<br /><br />You can get a copy of GIS Professional by following the link above.</p>
