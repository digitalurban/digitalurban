<p><a href="http://2.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/RxSAWYE2mOI/AAAAAAAAAuQ/JI4XKDdM7Yk/s1600-h/slweatherdisplay.jpg"><img style="margin: 0px auto 10px; display: block; text-align: center; cursor: pointer;" src="/assets/uploads/external/2.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/RxSAWYE2mOI/AAAAAAAAAuQ/JI4XKDdM7Yk/s400/slweatherdisplay.jpg" alt="" id="BLOGGER_PHOTO_ID_5121859798135380194" border="0" /></a><br />Displaying real-time data in Second Life is intriguing as it represents a direct cross over between the real world and the virtual. It also links in well with our current work with the <a href="http://www.ncess.ac.uk/">National Centre for E Social Science</a> and as such we now have a new sample real-time weather map in Second Life.<br /><br />Developed around users personal weather stations and the excellent <a href="http://www.weather-display.com/index.php">Weather Display</a> software Joel at our lab in <a href="http://www.casa.ucl.ac.uk/">CASA</a> has written a series of scripts allowing current wind gusts to be displayed in near real time.<br /><br />The data is refreshed  every 10 seconds resulting in the 3D bar chart morphing to the new value. Currently this is in the world as a test but we should have a more complete network in place later this week.<br /><br />If you have Second Life installed you can visit us by <a href="http://slurl.com/secondlife/Second%20Nature%202/202/192/117">clicking here</a> - you will get a red arrow pointing skywards, simply fly up to our section of Second Nature island.<br /><br />More data feeds relating to cities and things of a geographic nature in general to follow...</p>
