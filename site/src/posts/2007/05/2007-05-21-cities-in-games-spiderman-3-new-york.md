Available on multiple platforms -  Xbox 360, PlayStation 3, PC, Wii, PlayStation 2, Nintendo DS and Game Boy Advance - the game is based in New York and thus as is the nature of Spiderman games sees you webslinging your way around the city.<br /><br />In terms of city visualisation the genre is suited to high rises and long draw distances. As <a href="http://uk.gamespot.com/ps3/action/spiderman3/review.html">UK GameSpot</a> note in their review of the game - The city isn't gigantic, but there's enough familiar scenery around to make you want to explore, and that the city looks excellent is a big plus.
