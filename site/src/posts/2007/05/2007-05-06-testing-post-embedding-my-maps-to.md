The benefit of My Maps Plus is the ability to customise the look of your map and the elements you want to include.<br /><br />Embedding your map into Blogger, or any website, is easy - you simple copy your Google My Maps KML link and then choose various display options. My Maps Plus then creates a script that you cut and paste into your page.<br /><br />We will be working on a permanent map for the side bar, but for now the map above contains Video on the 'Audio and Perception of Urban Space' link, our current location, recent travels and Geolocated photographs using the Nokia N95.<br /><br />Double click to zoom in and out/ double click and drag to pan.<br /><br />Sure its a little squashed on the page but i think its usable...?</p>
