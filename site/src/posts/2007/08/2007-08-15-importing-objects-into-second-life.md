Made in 3D Studio Max the house was saved as a .obj, run through our in house software and saved as a Sculpt Map for import into our section of Second Life.<br /><br />We plan to make the software either freely or for a very small fee available once its complete. At the moment all the models appear in the preview window as intended yet once imported into Second Life they have a tendency to implode.<br /><br />Pictured below is the same house imploded:<br /><br /><a href="http://3.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/RsMUmVo4pgI/AAAAAAAAAk4/omJlxOsKxlM/s1600-h/Capture4.JPG"><img style="margin: 0px auto 10px; display: block; text-align: center; cursor: pointer;" src="/assets/uploads/external/3.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/RsMUmVo4pgI/AAAAAAAAAk4/omJlxOsKxlM/s400/Capture4.JPG" alt="" id="BLOGGER_PHOTO_ID_5098941851989747202" border="0" /></a><br />Joel in our lab is working through the issues, if you have any thoughts feel free to get in touch.<br /><br />If this works we may well have a simple route for importing 3d objects into Second Life.<br /><br />You can view more details on our work in Second Life and come by for a visit via details in <a href="http://digitalurban.blogspot.com/2007/08/global-and-local-data-representing.html">our previous posts</a>.</p>
