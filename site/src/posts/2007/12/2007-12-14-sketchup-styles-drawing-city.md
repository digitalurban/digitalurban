<p><a href="http://4.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/RnFDCRY7YZI/AAAAAAAAAaI/y2vrzyDeTl8/s1600-h/style1.JPG"><img style="margin: 0px auto 10px; display: block; text-align: center; cursor: pointer;" src="/assets/uploads/external/4.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/RnFDCRY7YZI/AAAAAAAAAaI/y2vrzyDeTl8/s400/style1.JPG" alt="" id="BLOGGER_PHOTO_ID_5075911961330016658" border="0" /></a><br />Google's SketchUp Pro version 6 includes the ability to visualise your 3D models using various 'Styles', as the <a href="http://www.sketchup.com/?sid=487">guide to the new features</a> explains  -<br /><br /><span style="font-style: italic;">"Styles are collections of display settings, including new options like Watermarks and Sketchy Effects, saved in the new Styles palette. You can create, save, and organize Styles as well</span><span style="font-style: italic;"> as share Styles with others. With a single click, you can quickly apply a style to any model by selecting it from the new Styles window"</span><br /><br />The use of Styles provides a unique way to visualise models within SketchUp, albeit with a slight performance hit.
