<p><a href="http://2.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/RoZKPW3ACaI/AAAAAAAAAcY/cTABEfrqneU/s1600-h/2bs.jpg"><img style="margin: 0pt 0pt 10px 10px; float: right; cursor: pointer;" src="/assets/uploads/external/2.bp.blogspot.com/_ADwvfqkxChw/RoZKPW3ACaI/AAAAAAAAAcY/cTABEfrqneU/s320/2bs.jpg" alt="" id="BLOGGER_PHOTO_ID_5081830857228945826" border="0" /></a>The ability to add a 'one shot' panoramic lens to a digital camera has been around for a while and although the combination works it is <span>unwieldy</span> to carry around. <span>Bubblescope</span> have taken the 'One Shot' lens method and built a camera around it - resulting in  world’s first standalone, single-shot panoramic digital                camera for capturing experiences as 360º x 104º.   Using <span>BubbleScope</span>® 1.0 software on a PC and                Mac the resulting images can be converted to <span>QTVR</span> movies.<br /><br /><span>Bubblescope</span> does seem to be to the simple solution for one click panoramas. The resolution maybe low(<span>ish</span>) and <span>the rig doesn't</span> <span>compete</span> with traditional <span>giga</span> pixel panoramas made with multiple images but for being out and about it captures the world in 360 degrees.<br /><br />To be honest we want one!<br /><br />Take a look at the <a href="http://www.bubblescope.com/"><span>BubbleScope</span></a> website for more info, the camera will be available to order shortly..</p>
