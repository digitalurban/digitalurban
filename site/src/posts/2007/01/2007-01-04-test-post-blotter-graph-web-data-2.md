<p>Using a service called  'Blotter' it is easy to embed your level of authroity from Technorati into you blog. We are not sure if this is a good idea or not but it does make for a quick and easy reference page so you can log your blogs growth or decline. <a href="http://www.dapper.net/dapplications/Blotter/">Blotter</a> is a new service from Dapper that graphs a blog's popularity over time. The popularity statistics are gathered from Technorati using the Dapper service.<br /><br />Stats are based on <a href="http://technorati.com/">Technorati Ranking</a> over time (start date 05/08/07):<br /><br /><a href="http://www.dapper.net/dapplications/Blotter"><img src="http://www.dapper.net/dapplications/BlogPlot/http%3A//www.digitalurban.blogspot.com" border="0" /></a><br /><br />See the <a href="http://www.dapper.net/dapplications/Blotter/">Blotter site</a> for how to embed your own graph - its just a case of cutting and pasting one line of html.<br /><br />See also <a href="http://technorati.com/weblog/2007/05/354.html">Technorati's guide to how it rates a blogs authority</a>.</p>
