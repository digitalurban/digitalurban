Picked up from <a href="http://www.cgarchitect.com/">CGarchitect</a> is a fascinating tutorial by Daarken on painting urban images in Photoshop.<br /><br /><a href="http://67.15.36.49/team/Tutorials_2/return_fire-escape/return-fire_escape_01.asp">Daarken's tutorial</a> provides a step-by-step walkthrough of creating the scene from starting with a blank canvas to ending up with the finished image.<br /><br />Of note is the starting point of empty document, no starting image or reference point, just a series of brush strokes and techniques to produce a image which approaches photorealisim.</p>
